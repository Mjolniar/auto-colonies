# Settings

## Quick version

- Empire panel (Intel screen, **Colony Autopilot** filter), **Settings**.
- 20 main rows in 6 groups. The rest is under **Advanced**.
- Most main rows are presets; Notes lists the settings each sets. **Custom**: no preset matches.
- **Reset to recommended** keeps Expansion style, War, New colonies, faction stances, Capital and Capital does.
- Rows for missing mods are hidden.

## Advanced

- **Budget:** Budget type **Automatic** (or Share of net income, Fixed amount); Per-project cap, New upkeep per month **Automatic**; Fixed cap; Fixed upkeep; From net income **50%**; From savings **15%**; Pause on loss **Off**; Convenience fees **Standard** (or Off, Low, High).
- **Surplus and money ladder:** Over 10,000,000 of surplus **Every colony**; Surplus terraforming **Max output**; Wonders and gates, Surplus research **On**; one row per rung: Stockpiles, Growth, Military, Economy, Wonders **On**, Shipyard and Expansion **Recommend only**; Shipyard stops at **6** unclaimed hulls.
- **Building:** Over-limit fix, Exports valued by market share, Take down unpaying industries, Endgame replaces industries **On**; Allow removals **Off**; Story points a month **2**.
- **AI cores and items:** Use AI cores **On**; Alpha cores **Only where removable**; Inspection risk **Never inspected**; Hegemony inspections **Advise**; Alphas in research buildings **First**; Research uses colony items **Off**.
- **Colony policies:** Hazard pay, Free port **Auto**; Hazard pay growth threshold **25%**; Hazard pay budget **30%** of net income; Senate edicts **On**.
- **People and diplomacy:** Administrator hiring, Assign administrators optimally **On**; Among equal stances, first **Worst relations first**; Stop raising relations at **Cooperative**; Recruit agents **On** (12,000 each, 60 days); Agents to keep **Auto** (2, or 3 with offense); Offense target (with Defense and offense); Autonomy **Recommend**.
- **War:** Emergency defense fleets, Retake your lost colonies **On** (Retake off on saves from 0.2.45); Top hostile threat as Rival, Coalition risk allowed **Off**; Conquered colonies start in **Auto**; Bombardment targets **Capitals only**.
- **Capital and storage:** Capital does **Deliveries and commerce**; Trade hub starts at **net income over 100,000**; Make room for trade buildings, Gather Storage at the capital **On**; Refit hub; Sell hub; Ships to the refit hub, Supplies, fuel, crew, marines too **Yes**.
- **Extras:** Stable locations, Trade contracts, Research, Special project supply, RAT settlement **Auto**; Megastructures, Station augments **Follow colony mode**; Measured mod buildings **Build when best**; Found when a site earns **20,000**; IndEvo specialties, VPC chips, Forge may use up chips, Courier shipping, Antimatter Stabilizers **On**.
- **New colonies:** Money for new colonies **Money ladder (Idle money and up)**; Where **Systems you hold**; Goal of a new colony **Fits its plan**; Method **Nexerelin colony fleet when possible**; Most colonies **Colony limit and administrators**; Fast buildout, Administrator past the limit **On**; TASC stations **Recommend only**.
- **Interface:** Announcement voice **Machine**; Button on the colony screen **On**.
