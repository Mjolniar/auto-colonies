# Research

This needs Ashes of the Domain (Vaults of Knowledge).

## How it picks research

- Research (Settings, Advanced, Extras) can be Auto, Advisor or Off. On Auto, it starts the best tech whenever nothing is running.
- A tech is worth what it unlocks, its own effects and half of the best tech it leads to. Techs a colony plan needs go first.
- Databanks are saved for plan techs, but after 90 days the best tech you can afford goes ahead.
- Research uses colony items can be Off, From storage or From storage and buildings. AI cores go in research buildings first and cut their costs.

## Surplus research

- It builds extra Research Facilities at Auto colonies, and each one past the first adds +10% research speed, up to 8. It starts at the Surplus money tier ([Money](money.md#economy)).
- Once the Black Site opens and you don't have one, it upgrades one Research Facility at an Auto colony with stability 10 or more.

## Special projects and wonders

- If black site projects or production orders need a good none of your colonies make, it plans a producer at the best colony.
- It builds the Daedalus Array and the Bifrost network once your colonies make every good each one needs ([Money](money.md#wonders)).
