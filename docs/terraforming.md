# Terraforming

Needs Terraforming Made Easy (TME). Boggled's TASC is at the bottom.

## Quick version

- A colony plan can aim for a TME special world: Forge, Fortress, Paradise, Hive, Death or Waste.
- Auto colonies run TME projects, one or two steps ahead.
- Surplus terraforming spends spare credits at Auto colonies.

## Special worlds

- A world is picked only if the colony can reach it and its industries fit the max-size slots. Steps follow TME's requirements. The Unification Center goes last.
- No reachable world: a normal plan, plus hazard clean-up where needed.
- After paid terraforming toward one world, a colony switches to a plan that undoes it only if that plan is worth at least twice as much. Removing a paid-for condition as a side effect counts as undoing it.

## The plan's terraforming

- Up to 40% of the monthly budget. Checks planner savings before spending.
- Scores hazards a project adds. Re-plans when conditions change.
- A 360-day structure takes the construction slot only when no better build is waiting.

## Surplus terraforming

Best value per credit first: richer deposits, better farmland, cleared hazards. It builds the structure if missing and takes it down when done.

- **Surplus terraforming** (Settings, Advanced, Surplus and money ladder): **Max output** (recommended; 1,000 a month or more after upkeep), **Pays back in 10 years** (set by the Thrifty preset), or **Off**.
- Starts from the money tier Surplus up, with the Economy rung On ([Money](money.md#economy)).
- A sequence that pays back within 24 months can use the monthly budget, within the 40% terraforming share and free cash.
- Spending freely: up to three projects per colony at once.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

Projects are valued by the conditions they leave. Auto starts the best. The colony panel lists the best four with Start buttons. Mining and siphon stations: [Supported mods](supported-mods.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled).
