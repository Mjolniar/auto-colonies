# Getting started

## Modes

- Colonies start in Advisor, which ranks builds beside the build menu. Approve proposes builds every 60 days, and Auto builds within your budget.
- The first-time setup sets your Empire mode, and any setting you change yourself stays.
- Click Colony Autopilot on the colony screen to open the colony panel. The Log page shows what it did and why.

## First-time setup

- Pick Full expansion or [Low profile](low-profile.md), and a goal ([How it builds](how-it-builds.md)). Balanced is the default.
- Spending: Normal (50% of net income and 15% of savings a month) is recommended.
- New colonies: Off is recommended ([New colonies](expansion.md)).
- War (Nexerelin): Retaliate is recommended ([War and security](war-and-security.md)).
- Credit reserve: A quarter of your credits, between 50,000 and 500,000.
- Every row is in [Settings](settings.md).

## Fast buildout

Your first colony gets the offer, with a credit floor of 250,000, 100,000, 500,000 or 1,000,000. It spends the credits above the floor on whatever pays back fastest and stops after 30 days in a row of profit.

## Troubleshooting

If Auto builds nothing, check the budget line on the Colonies page. It's zero when you're under the reserve, when Pause on loss is on, or when net income is negative and Budget type is Share of net income.
