# Your capital

## Which colony

- The capital gets picked by production gathering point, then Storage contents, then size and accessibility. You can change it on a colony panel or in Capital (Settings, Items and people), and None turns it off.
- The Capital does setting (Settings, Advanced, Capital and storage) can be Deliveries and commerce (default), Deliveries only or Off.

## Deliveries

- Custom production arrives at the capital, and that includes [Shipyard](money.md#the-money-ladder) orders.
- Every week, ships, weapons, wings, blueprints, specs, supplies, fuel, crew and marines in the storage tabs get sent there for the cost of a shipment plus 1,000 a ship. A refit or sell hub ([Items](items-and-cores.md#storage-logistics)) takes priority.

## Trade buildings

- They start once net income is over the Trade hub starts at setting (100,000 a month, or 50,000, 250,000, Right away), as long as no fast buildout is running.
- They go in this order: Spaceport, Megaport, Commerce, Trade Outpost, Bazaar, Requisitions Centre, Union HQ, Salvage Yards, Underworld, Waystation.
- The costly ones need the Surplus tier, the [Economy rung](money.md#economy) On and upkeep within 5% of colony net.
- The capital gets a Battlestation from size 5 and a Star Fortress from 7.
- From Idle money with the slots full, it'll replace the weakest ordinary industry when the swap pays, once per 90 days at most.
