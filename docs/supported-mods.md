# Supported mods

## Quick version

- Requirements: none. Support turns on when the mod is enabled.
- Diagnostics (empire panel): status per mod.
- Scope: Auto colonies only ([Modes](how-it-builds.md#modes)). [Issues](https://github.com/Mjolniar/auto-colonies/issues).

## Mods

- [Terraforming Made Easy](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29441.0): [Terraforming](terraforming.md). Beta core for relay, buoy, array upgrades ([Stable locations](how-it-builds.md#stable-locations)).
- [Industrial Evolution](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=18011.0): ambassadors (30 days), fitting edicts, VPC chips, mining and refining specialty, Courier Port for shortages. Harsh edicts not automated.
- [Nexerelin](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9175.0): threat, sieges, police crisis, Derelict Empire start. [War and security](war-and-security.md). [Money](money.md#autonomy-nexerelin). [New colonies](expansion.md): expedition at 165,000 (100 points) or 205,000 (150). Untested: [patch notes](changelog.md#0300---2026-11-11).
- [Ashes of the Domain, VoK, ToT](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26307.0), [Building Menu Overhaul](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=31308.0): locked buildings skipped. Scored by effect. Trade contracts at 15% spare. [Research](research.md). Left to you: Grand Wonders except the Daedalus Array, Star Citadels, custom production.
- [Emergent Threats (IX Revival)](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=27284.0): Antimatter Stabilizer upgrades Fuel Production. Panopticon goes into the Surveillance Center.
- [Random Assortment of Things](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26260.0): Frontiers settlement at a 20,000 a month site. Refining and Trading Post at 2-year payback.
- [Station Augments](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=30583.0): output while calm, combat under high threat. Refit 7 days.
- [Interstellar Imperium](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=8007.0), [Knights of Ludd](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29124.0): stations count like vanilla.
- [Grand.Colonies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=20986.0): without it, 12 buildings maximum.
- [Substance.Abuse](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24378.0): Brewery needs a recipe in Storage.
- [Volkov Industrial Conglomerate](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=19603.0): viral contamination treatment first. Soil nanites on vanilla farmland.
- [Roider Union](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9547.0): Dives and Union HQ valued by deposits. Rockpiper Shipworks after its blueprint.
- [Planetary Shield: Access Control](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14990.0): valued as ground defense plus lower Pather interest.
- [Unknown Skies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29876.0), [What We Left Behind](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=25205.0), [Astral Ascension](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=28273.0): mining conditions and water worlds counted.
- [Niko's Scavengable Industries](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36195.0): learned blueprints built when they pay.
- [Junk Yard Dogs](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22826.0), [Carter's Freetraders](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24433.0): forges and Biolab built normally.
- [Vulpoids](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29929.0): Organ Farms and Biotech upgrade when they pay. Colony items placed by effect.
- [Yunru Industries, Slightly Better Tech-Mining, Yunru's Worlds](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=21968.0): valued by effect. Bazaar only at your [capital](capital.md).
- [United Aurora Federation](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=12709.0): Dimensional Nanoforge, Servosync Pump, Modular Purifier, Garrison Transmitter placed by effect.
- [Auto Outfitting](https://github.com/Mjolniar/auto-outfitting): big weapon orders use AotD production orders.
- [Kadur Remnant](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35145.0): Kadur Majority Faith's +1 output on industrial buildings (size 3 and up) and Virus Bombed's -2 on every output line count in build values.
- [JaydeePiracy](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36201.0): Quarry Lakes (Refining needs no ore), Domain-era Meshugatron (Fuel Production +3 fuel) and Eutrophicated Biosphere (Farming adds organics) count in build values. Planetkiller Launch Site and the planetkiller item left to you.
- [Corrupt.Officials](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35125.0): extra income at a colony above the cutoff counts at the share the colony keeps. Settings read from LunaLib.
- [Tahlan Shipworks](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14935.0): no new colony on a Kassadari Claim planet.
- [Gladiator Society](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=33586.0): no new colony on The Gulf.
- [Scy Nation](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29535.0), [CJY's Toy Box](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35093.0), [AI-Retrofits](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22261.0): Intelligence Command Center, Private Production Facility, both greenhouses and Population Replacement Center left to you.
- [Blue](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14041.0), [Oculian Armada](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9035.0): Lanestate Battleyards and Orbital Matrix count as ship producers for the production floors.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=17094.0)

- Terraforming projects: valued and started. [Terraforming](terraforming.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled)
- Mining and siphon stations: founded once researched, with colony-limit room. 60 days, TASC costs plus a fee.
- TASC stations: recommended only while New colonies is Auto and Expansion is Recommend only or Off.

## Omega Core Everywhere and Astral Ascension Renewed

[Omega Core Everywhere](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36168.0) | [Astral Ascension Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36167.0)

- Astral Ascension Renewed: an Omega core can govern a colony. 10 inspection points.

## Rogue Planets

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36337.0)

- Synergy pairs: Degenerate Volatiles, Diamond Core, Radiolytic Catalyst, Hollowed Caldera Garrisons, Vacation Paradise & Resort, Smugglers' Haven, Viable Slave Labor, Magmatic Antimatter Forge and Ground-to-Space Missile Defense count in build values.
- Other conditions: Hydrocarbon Fractionation, Megalithic Outcrops, Clandestine Harvesting Syndicate and Pilgrim World count in build values. Upkeep conditions count in every upkeep.
- Fragile Biosphere: no industry but Farming is built or planned.
- Volatile Additive Reactor: the second of Refining and Fuel Production is left to you. A running pair is not taken down, replaced or upgraded.
- Stability: Viable Slave Labor and Supercooled Cryo-Sinks colonies are held at 6 or more. At the second month of slave labor unrest, Commerce is taken down if that lifts stability above 5.
- Pilgrim World: no AI cores and no AI administrator.
- Policies: hazard pay stays off while Diamond Core runs. The free port costs no stability while Smugglers' Haven runs.
- Production floors: Degenerate Volatiles, Ship Graveyard and Magmatic Antimatter Forge colonies rank first as floor hosts. The Magmatic forge counts at the production gathering point.
- New colonies: Antimatter Anomaly fuel and crustquake goods count. Fragile Biosphere counts farmland only.
- Left to you: Forgotten Vault-World's Tech-Mining, the Trash King's seat, the Violet Dwarf system, the Afterlight. Cold Sky Observatory and Resonant Signal Trap get no build value.
- Colony panel: Mod features lists each condition at the colony and how Autopilot handles it.

## Any other mod

- Deposits, colony items: handled like vanilla. Buildings with no formula: measured on a test colony ([How it builds](how-it-builds.md#mod-buildings)). Unsupported mods: left alone.
