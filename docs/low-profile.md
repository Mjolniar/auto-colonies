# Low profile

## Quick version
- Optional expansion style: **Settings, Playstyle, Expansion style**. Colonies replan right away.
- It keeps Hegemony inspection risk, Pather interest and crisis progress low, and earns less money.

## What it changes
- **Crises:** crisis progress is valued at 10x normal. Patrol HQ, Military Base and High Command slow it and are worth 10x more.
- **Hegemony:** no AI cores above size 3. Existing ones go to Storage, one a day. A free human administrator replaces an AI core one at 4+ stability if the colony can lose the core's +1. Omega core administrators stay.
- **Pathers:** nothing is added that takes a size 4+ colony to 7 Pather interest. Items over it go to Storage, least useful first, at colonies at or above the stability floor. Buildings stay.
- **Tri-Tachyon:** one competing good is free. More must be worth it.
- **Diktat:** a building making 4+ fuel is priced for the Diktat's interest.
- **Luddic Church:** buildings that prevent a Luddic majority get credit. Patrol HQ does not count.
- **Free ports:** off. They draw Nexerelin's police.
- **War:** War level capped at Defend. No agent sabotage. Inspections are not resisted.
- **New colonies:** only in systems you hold ([New colonies](expansion.md)), none next to a Remnant nexus.
- **Omega and IX cores:** not used.

## When each rule ends

| Rule | Ends when you |
|---|---|
| Hegemony | beat 3 inspections or join the Persean League |
| Luddic Path | beat their attack or pay the tithe |
| Tri-Tachyon | raid their commerce |
| Diktat | beat their attack or take their deal |
| Luddic Church | beat the Knights or take the deal |

The Crises page shows which are on.

## Limits
- **Pirates** grow with your biggest colony. Military goes up sooner. Beating one big raid ends pirate crises for good.
- **Persean League:** at a size 5 colony it blockades you until you join (your call).
