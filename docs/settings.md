# Settings

- With LunaLib, the settings are in its menu (F3 by default) under Auto Colonies, with the main rows on the General tab and the rest on the Advanced tab. Without it, they're on the colony panel under Empire, then Settings.
- The empire mode sets the defaults ([Mode defaults](#mode-defaults)). A row you change shows "set by you" and keeps its value when the empire mode changes.
- Reset to recommended puts every setting back to the empire mode's defaults, except your Expansion style, faction stances, capital pick and focus world.
- Most main rows are presets, where Mode default follows the empire mode and Custom means no preset matches.
- Rows for mods you don't have are hidden, or show "Needs" and the mod's name in LunaLib.
- There are no cheat or balance settings. Cheats are in Easy Sector.

## LunaLib

- LunaLib is optional. Its values are global, and changes you save in its menu apply to the loaded game and count as set by you.
- Story mode follows LunaLib's value in every save, but LunaLib keeps your value for a setting that story mode is holding at off.
- With LunaLib, the Settings page only has this save's picks (Capital, Refit hub, Sell hub, Offense target) and Faction stances.
- When LunaLib holds settings from a save you've set up, a new save's first Approve or Auto colony keeps them and doesn't change the empire mode.

## Main rows

The defaults are marked (recommended).

- Story mode: It's off by default ([Story mode](story-mode.md)).
- Empire mode: Off, Advisor (new saves), Approve or Auto.
- Expansion style: Full expansion or [Low profile](low-profile.md).
- Spending: Thrifty, Normal (recommended) or Lavish. The net income share is 30% / 50% / 80%, the savings share is 5% / 15% / 30% and the new upkeep cap is 5% / 10% / 20% of net income.
- Extras: Auto (recommended), Recommend only or Off.
- [New colonies](expansion.md): Off, Ask first or Auto (by empire mode).
- War (Nexerelin): Off, Defend, Retaliate (Auto mode), Hold them down, Conquer or Scorched ([War page](war-and-security.md#the-war-page)).
- Credit reserve: An amount (recommended: a quarter of credits, 50,000-500,000). It isn't spent.
- Surplus investment: Off, over 1M, over 3M (recommended), over 5M or over 10M ([Focus world](money.md)).
- What to buy: Balanced (recommended), Fleet, Expansion, Economy or Save ([Money ladder](money.md#the-money-ladder)).
- Endgame: From 1,000,000/month (recommended), 2,000,000, 500,000 or Off. It builds for production and defense.
- Story points: Yours only (recommended), One per colony, 2 a month or 5 a month.
- Stations: Pick by score (recommended), Low tech, Midline, High tech, Imperium, Diable Avionics, Roider Union or Only where they pay. It asks once before the first station ([How it builds](how-it-builds.md)).
- Take down buildings: Never, Clean up (recommended) or Free hand.
- Colony policies: Auto (recommended) or Don't touch.
- AI cores: Off, Safe (recommended), Small colonies only, Defended or No limit.
- Administrators: Off, Place only or Hire and place (recommended).
- Capital: Autopilot picks (recommended), one of your colonies or None ([Your capital](how-it-builds.md#your-capital)).
- Agents (Nexerelin): Off, Defense (recommended) or Defense and offense.
- Ambassadors (IndEvo): Hire and install (recommended) or Off.
- Faction stances: It's an OPEN button for the [Factions page](war-and-security.md#faction-stances).
- On-screen log: Every entry (recommended), Important only or Off.

## Advanced

- Budget: Budget type is Automatic, From net income is 50%, From savings is 15%, Pause on loss is Off and Convenience fees are Standard.
- Building: Over-limit fix is On, Allow removals is Off and Story points a month is 2.
- AI cores: Use AI cores is On, Alpha cores is Only where removable, Inspection risk is Never inspected and Hegemony inspections is Advise.
- Colony policies: Hazard pay growth threshold is 25%, Hazard pay budget is 30% of net income and Senate edicts is On.
- Agents: Recruit agents is On (12,000 each, 60 days) and Agents to keep is Auto (2, or 3 with offense).
- War: Emergency defense fleets, Retake your lost colonies and [Patrol kills count for crises](war-and-security.md#colony-crises) are On, and Bombardment targets is Capitals only.
- [Enforcer patrols](war-and-security.md#enforcer-patrols) (War group, works without Nexerelin) are Off, and Sindrian Diktat raiders is On.
- Capital: The Trade hub starts at net income over 100,000.
- Extras: Found when a site earns is 20,000, Measured mod buildings is Build when best, and Station augments and Megastructures are on Follow colony mode.
- New colonies: Method is Nexerelin colony fleet when possible.
- Production: Production is Light, Hull sources is 1, Hull capacity is 100,000 a month, Order goods within is 6 months, Production quality is 0.8 and Parts reserve is 100.
- Floors: Supplies and fuel floors are No shortage, Floors at Money colonies is Off and Floor builds first is On.
- Taper and copies: Money taper is Gentle, Same building copies is 2 and the next copy counts 75%.
