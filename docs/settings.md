# Settings

## Quick version

- Empire panel (Intel screen, **Colony Autopilot** filter), **Settings**. Click a group to open it.
- **Use recommended settings** resets everything in one click. They're meant to work if you only ever switch colonies to Auto.
- Groups for mods you don't have don't show up.

## Expansion style

- **Expansion style** (asked at your first colony): Full expansion or Low profile. Low profile keeps growing but stays under the radar. See [Low profile](low-profile.md).

## Budget and spending

- **Budget type** (Automatic): or Share of net income, or Fixed. Automatic spends half of last month's net income plus 15% of credits above the reserve.
- **From net income / From savings** (50% / 15%): the two halves of Automatic.
- **Per-project cap** (about monthly net income, 1M-5M): anything over is proposed to you.
- **Credit reserve** (a quarter of your credits, 50,000-500,000): never spent.
- **New upkeep cap per month** (Automatic): 10% of last month's net income, at least 20,000, or a fixed amount.
- **Surplus investment** (over 3M): Off, or over 1M, 3M, 5M or 10M. Credits past the line go into a focus world. See [Money](money.md).
- **Over 10,000,000 of surplus** (Every colony spends it): or Focus world only.
- **Spending freely** starts at 10M of surplus and stays on until it drops under 7M.
- **Endgame economy** (from 1M a month): 2M, 500k, or Off. Builds for production, research and military once you're spending freely.
- **Endgame replaces industries** (On): lets the endgame swap weak industries.
- **Wonders and gates while spending freely**, **Surplus terraforming** (Max output) and **Surplus research**: for Ashes of the Domain, TME and Vaults of Knowledge.
- **Pause on loss** (Off): stops spending while net income is negative.
- **Convenience fees** (Standard): Off, Low, Standard or High.

## Building and planning

- **Station line** (Pick by score): Low tech, Midline, High tech, or Imperium.
- **Stable locations** (Auto): Recommend only, or Off. See [How it builds](how-it-builds.md#stable-locations).
- **Allow removals** (Off): lets it remove buildings to replace them.
- **Mod buildings with measured effects** (Build when best): Recommend only, or Ignore.
- **Story points** (Player only): or One per colony, or Monthly cap (with its own cap row).
- **Fix colonies over the industry limit** (Yes): an Auto colony over its slots takes down its weakest industry, one a day. Never one that holds up its stability.
- **Take down industries that stopped paying** (Yes): one worth less than its upkeep for 30 days goes. Never a locked one, a plan step, or anything that adds stability.
- **Station at every colony** (Yes): see [How it builds](how-it-builds.md#plans).
- **Exports valued by market share** (On): Off gives every unit one flat rate. See [How it builds](how-it-builds.md#how-it-picks).

## Items and AI cores

- **Use AI cores** (On): cores only come from Autopilot Storage.
- **Alpha cores** (Only where removable): or Anywhere, or Never.
- **Inspection risk** (Never inspected): More where defended, Small colonies only, or No limit.
- **Hegemony inspection orders** (Advise): Resist where defended, or Off.

## Capital

See [Your capital](capital.md).

- **Capital** (Autopilot picks): or one of your colonies.
- **Capital does** (Deliveries and commerce): Deliveries only, or Off.
- **Commerce starts** (net income over 100,000), **Make room for trade buildings** (Yes) and **Gather your Storage there** (Yes).

## Administrators and ambassadors

- **Administrator hiring** (On) and **Assign administrators optimally** (On): or Off.
- **Ambassadors** (Hire and install): or Off (Industrial Evolution).
- **Ambassador focus** (Worst relations first), **Stop raising a faction at** (Cooperative) and **Per faction** (High, Normal or Ignore) tune who gets them.

## Colony policies

- **Hazard pay** (Auto): or Don't touch. Goes where it speeds growth most.
- **Free port** (Auto), **Senate edicts** (Enact) and **Emergency defense fleets** (Send when threatened): or Off.
- **War fleets** (Protect and strike back): Protect, or Off (Nexerelin). See [War and security](war-and-security.md).

## Mod integrations

- **Agents** (Defense): Off, or Defense and offense (Nexerelin). **Recruit agents** (On; 12,000 each, 60 days). **Agents to keep** (Auto: 2, or 3 with offense). **Offense target** (Whoever attacks you).
- **Autonomy** (Recommend): Off or Auto (Nexerelin). Money only, never for the colony limit or new colonies. See [Money](money.md#autonomy-nexerelin).
- **Industrial Evolution:** Specialized Mining and Refining, VPC chips, Commodity Forge chips and Courier Port shipping are all on at their recommended values.
- **Settlement** (Random Assortment of Things): **Settlement mode** (Auto) and **Found when a site makes at least** (20,000 a month).
- **Ashes of the Domain:** **Trade contracts** (Auto), **Research** (Auto), **Research spends colony items (surplus only)** (Off, see [Research](research.md#techs-that-take-colony-items)), **Megastructures** (Follow colony mode).

## Interface

- **Button on the colony screen** (On): or Off.
- **Log entries on screen** (Every entry): Important only, or Off.
- **Announcement voice** (Machine): Plain. Machine puts a short code in front, like `BUILD / Hot Springs: Planetary Shield (750k¢)`. The Log has the full sentence either way.

There are no cheat or balance settings. The mod manages your colonies the way you could yourself.

## Coming in 3.0

- **Back up your saves before updating.** A save made on 3.0 won't load in 2.45.x.
- **About 20 main rows** (planned), with the rest under an **Advanced** section. Each main row is a preset that sets several of today's settings and shows **Custom** when you've mixed them. Planned spending presets: Thrifty (30% / 5%), Normal (50% / 15%, today), Lavish (80% / 30%).
- **Rows that merge or go** (planned): War fleets becomes one War level (Retaliate is today's default), per-faction High/Normal/Ignore becomes a stance per faction, and the per-project cap follows your income. The admin delay switch is already gone.
- **Shorter text and first-time setup** (planned): five rows (Spending, Extras, New colonies, War, Credit reserve), everything else recommended.
- **Your settings carry over** (already built, being tested), and nothing gets more aggressive on its own.

Plans can still change before release.
