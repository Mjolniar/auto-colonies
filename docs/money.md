# Money, surplus and the endgame

## The budget

- You set the budget with Spending and in Settings, Advanced, Budget ([preset values](settings.md#playstyle)). Automatic (recommended) spends a share of colony net income and of credits above the reserve, and unspent budget carries over for six months.

## Money tiers

Free cash is your credits minus the reserve, unpaid custom production and 2 months of upkeep and fleet costs. N is colony net income, taken as a 3-month median.

| Tier | Starts at (free cash) | Ladder spends |
|---|---|---|
| Tight | below Surplus | 0% |
| Surplus | max(1,000,000, 2 x N) | 20% |
| Idle money | max(3,000,000, 5 x N) | 35% |
| Rich | max(10,000,000, 15 x N) | 60% |
| Endgame | Rich, with N at the Endgame line | 80% |

## Money taper

- A build's money counts in full up to 300,000 a month, then 60% up to 1,000,000 and 35% above that. Money taper (Settings, Advanced, Production) can be Off, Gentle (recommended) or Strong (200,000 and 600,000, 50% and 25%).

## The money ladder

- Each month, the tier's share of free cash is split over the rungs in What to buy order. The shares are Stockpiles 10%, Growth 20%, Military 20%, Shipyard 25%, [Expansion](expansion.md) 15%, Economy 10% and Wonders the rest.
- Military and Expansion open at Idle money and Wonders at Rich. Shipyard opens at Surplus with Production on, or at Idle money with it Off.
- Each rung (Settings, Advanced, Surplus and money ladder) can be On, Recommend only or Off, and Shipyard and Expansion default to Recommend only.
- Shipyard orders a known-blueprint hull a month from your Doctrine priority list (plus a 2% fee) for delivery to the [capital](how-it-builds.md#your-capital).
- Military pays for upgrades at Auto colonies, stations a size early and non-emergency defense fleets.

### Economy

- [Surplus research](research.md#surplus-research), [surplus terraforming](terraforming.md#surplus-terraforming) and the capital's costly [trade buildings](how-it-builds.md#trade-buildings) need this rung On.

### Wonders

- The Daedalus Array, Bifrost gates and endgame industry swaps need this rung On.

## Surplus investment

- Credits over the Surplus investment line (recommended 3,000,000) go to the Auto colony with the most to build.
- At Rich, once surplus credits reach half the line (1,500,000), every Approve and Auto colony spends from the surplus.

## The endgame economy

- At one calm Auto colony at a time, it puts up the best tier 4 station you can build (an AotD Star Citadel, or the Imperium's Stella Castellum), and the old station keeps working until it's done.

- From the Endgame tier, an Auto colony with full slots swaps its weakest industry for one worth 10,000 a month more, up to once a month.

## Autonomy (Nexerelin)

- Autonomy (Settings, Advanced, People and diplomacy) can be Off, Recommend (recommended) or Auto. An autonomous colony pays you a quarter of its profit or loss and sits outside the colony limit.
- It's used for colonies a year old or more with three straight losing months, or in a money emergency. A colony you reject isn't offered again for a year.
