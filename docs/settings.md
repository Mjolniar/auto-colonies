# Settings

Settings live in the empire panel (Intel screen, **Colony Autopilot** filter, **Settings**). Click a group to open it. Every row shows what's recommended next to it, and **Use recommended settings** puts everything back in one click. The recommended settings are meant to work if you only ever switch colonies to Auto, so if you're not sure about something, leave it on the recommendation.

Groups for mods you don't have don't show up.

## Budget and spending

- **Budget type** - Automatic, Share of net income, or Fixed. Automatic (recommended) spends half of last month's net income plus 15% of your credits above the reserve each month.
- **From net income / From savings** - the two halves of the Automatic budget (recommended 50% and 15%).
- **Per-project cap** - anything over this is proposed to you instead of built. Recommended around your monthly net income, 1M-5M.
- **Credit reserve** - never spent by anything. Recommended a quarter of your credits, 50,000-500,000.
- **New upkeep cap per month** - Automatic (10% of last month's net income, at least 20,000) or a fixed amount.
- **Surplus investment** - Off, or over 1M, 3M (recommended), 5M or 10M. Credits past the line go into a focus world. See [Money](money.md).
- **Over 10,000,000 of surplus** - Every colony spends it (recommended), or Focus world only.
- **Endgame economy** - from 1M a month (recommended), 2M, 500k, or Off. Once spending freely with that much income, it builds for production, research and military instead of money.
- **Endgame replaces industries** - On (recommended) or Off. Lets the endgame swap weak industries for better ones.
- **Wonders and gates while spending freely** - On or Off (Ashes of the Domain).
- **Surplus terraforming** - Max output (recommended), Pays back in 10 years, or Off (TME).
- **Surplus research** - On or Off. More Research Facilities from the surplus (Vaults of Knowledge).
- **Pause on loss** - stops spending while net income is negative. Recommended off.
- **Convenience fees** - Off, Low, Standard (recommended) or High.

## Building and planning

- **Station line** - Pick by score (recommended), Low tech, Midline, High tech, or Imperium (Interstellar Imperium).
- **Stable locations** - Auto (recommended), Recommend only, or Off. Builds comm relays, nav buoys and sensor arrays at stable spots in systems with your Auto colonies (and TME's Domain-era upgrade, AotD's Hypershunt Receivers). See [How it builds](how-it-builds.md#stable-locations).
- **Allow removals** - lets it remove buildings to replace them. Recommended off, that's your call. Terraforming structures it built itself and the endgame's replacements don't need this.
- **Mod buildings with measured effects** - Build when best (recommended), Recommend only, or Ignore.
- **Story points** - Player only (recommended), One per colony, or Monthly cap.
- **Story point monthly cap** - how many story points a month, with Monthly cap.

## Items and AI cores

- **Use AI cores** - On (recommended) or Off. Cores only come from Autopilot Storage.
- **Alpha cores** - Only where removable (recommended), Anywhere, or Never.
- **Alpha cores in research buildings** - First (recommended) or By value (Vaults of Knowledge).
- **Inspection risk** - Never inspected (recommended), More where defended, Small colonies only, or No limit.
- **Hegemony inspection orders** - Advise (recommended), Resist where defended, or Off.
- **Special project supply** - Auto (recommended), Warn only, or Off (Vaults of Knowledge).
- **Antimatter Stabilizers to Advanced Fuel Production** - Convert or Off (Emergent Threats). Needs Allow removals.

## Administrators and ambassadors

- **Administrator hiring** - On (recommended) or Off.
- **Assign administrators optimally** - On (recommended) or Off.
- **Ambassadors** - Hire and install (recommended) or Off (Industrial Evolution).
- **Ambassador focus** - Worst relations first (recommended), Strongest factions, Nearest neighbors, or Priority list only.
- **Stop raising a faction at** - Welcoming, Friendly, Cooperative (recommended), or Never stop.
- **Per faction** - High, Normal or Ignore for each faction.

## Colony policies

- **Hazard pay** - Auto (recommended) or Don't touch. Goes to the colonies it speeds up the most.
- **Hazard pay where growth is at least** - how much faster a colony has to grow to get it. Recommended 25%.
- **Hazard pay limit, all colonies** - its total, as a share of net income. Recommended 30%.
- **Free port** - Auto (recommended) or Don't touch.
- **Senate edicts** - Enact (recommended) or Off (Industrial Evolution).
- **Emergency defense fleets** - Send when threatened (recommended) or Off (Nexerelin).
- **War fleets** - Protect and strike back (recommended), Protect, or Off (Nexerelin). See [War and security](war-and-security.md).

## Agents (Nexerelin)

- **Agents** - Off, Defense (recommended), or Defense and offense.
- **Recruit agents** - On (recommended; 12,000 each, 60 days to report for duty) or Off.
- **Agents to keep** - Auto (2, or 3 with offense) or a number.
- **Offense target** - Whoever attacks you (recommended), A random enemy, or a faction at war with you. Only with Defense and offense.

## Autonomy (Nexerelin)

- **Autonomy** - Off, Recommend (recommended), or Auto. Money only, never for the colony limit or new colonies. See [Money](money.md#autonomy-nexerelin).

## Industry automation (Industrial Evolution)

- **Specialized Mining and Refining** - Switch to best (recommended) or Off.
- **VPC chips** - Install (recommended) or Off.
- **Commodity Forge may use up chips** - Yes (recommended) or No.
- **Supply shipping (Courier Port)** - Cover shortages (recommended) or Off.

## Settlement (Random Assortment of Things)

- **Settlement mode** - Advisor, Approve, Auto (recommended), or Off.
- **Found when a site makes at least** - recommended 20,000 a month.

## Station augments (Station Augments)

- **Station augments** - Follow colony mode (recommended), Recommend only, or Off.
- **Station construction** (Terraforming & Station Construction) - Recommend only (default), Auto, or Off. Founds TASC mining and siphon stations as new colonies in calm or well-defended systems.

## Ashes of the Domain

- **Trade contracts** - Advisor, Auto (recommended), or Off.
- **Research** - Advisor, Auto (recommended), or Off.
- **Research spends colony items (surplus only)** - Off (recommended), From storage, or From storage and buildings. See [Research](research.md#techs-that-take-colony-items).
- **Megastructures (Hypershunt, Pluto, Nidavelir)** - Follow colony mode (recommended), Recommend only, or Off.

## Interface

- **Button on the colony screen** - On (recommended) or Off.
- **Log entries on screen** - Every entry (recommended, each as one short line), Important only, or Off.
- **Announcement voice** - Machine (recommended) or Plain. Machine shows each entry on screen as a short readout, like `AP\\ #HOT_SPRINGS :: BUILD // Planetary Shield // 750k¢`, plus a ping now and then about what it's weighing (a colony's scan, inspection points), at most once a week each. Plain is the old "Autopilot: ..." sentence. The Log page has the full sentence either way.

There are no cheat or balance settings. The mod only manages your colonies the way you could yourself.
