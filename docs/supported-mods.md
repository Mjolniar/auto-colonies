# Supported mods

## Quick version
- No mod here is required. Support for each turns on when it is enabled, in any combination.
- **Diagnostics** (empire panel) shows each mod's status: OK, newer version, partly off, off.
- Unless noted, only Auto colonies are acted on ([Modes](how-it-builds.md#modes)). Everything is paid from the budget.
- Support requests: open an [issue](https://github.com/Mjolniar/auto-colonies/issues).

## Terraforming Made Easy

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29441.0). Special-world plans, plan terraforming and surplus terraforming: [Terraforming](terraforming.md). Its comm relay, nav buoy and sensor array upgrade uses a spare Beta core ([Stable locations](how-it-builds.md#stable-locations)).

## Industrial Evolution

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=18011.0)

- **Ambassadors:** hired for an empty Embassy or installed from Storage; 30 days to arrive. Priority follows the [faction stance](war-and-security.md#faction-stances).
- **Senate edicts:** the one that suits the colony. Harsh ones (forced labour, cleansing, single-child, labour limits) are not automated.
- **VPC chips:** installed from Storage. **Specialized Mining and Refining:** the best specialty.
- **Supply shipping:** a monthly Courier Port contract covers a shortage, then cancels. Only the best-connected colony gets a Courier Port.

## Nexerelin

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9175.0). Covers threat, sieges, the police crisis and the Derelict Empire start. Player outposts are left alone.

- **War, diplomacy, agents:** [War and security](war-and-security.md). **Autonomy:** [Money](money.md#autonomy-nexerelin).
- **Colony fleets:** Auto-expansion uses a Nexerelin colony expedition when fleet request points are spare (165,000 at 100 points, 205,000 at 150). The colony counts toward your colony limit ([New colonies](expansion.md)).
- **Not tested in a running game yet:** 0.3.0.0 passed the offline checks and a short in-game test on one save. Untested parts: player-faction saturation bombardment (Nexerelin has no fleet request type for it), player colony fleets, conquered-colony rebellions. See the [patch notes](changelog.md#not-tested-in-a-running-game-yet).

## Ashes of the Domain

[Ashes of the Domain, VoK, ToT](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26307.0) | [Building Menu Overhaul](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=31308.0)

- Nothing locked or hidden is built. Upgrades start the way the menu does it.
- Starter buildings, synergies and management styles are scored by their real effects.
- Research, special project supply, wonders: [Research](research.md).
- **Trade contracts:** only when ToT's numbers leave 15% spare and it beats exporting.
- **Hypershunt Receivers:** at stable spots once researched, with a Coronal Hypershunt Tap in Storage.
- **Megastructures** (Coronal Hypershunt, Pluto Mining Station, Nidavelir Complex): anything you change waits for **Let Autopilot manage it**.
- **Left to you:** Grand Wonders other than the Daedalus Array, Star Citadels, custom production, management style.

## Emergent Threats (IX Revival)

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=27284.0). An Antimatter Stabilizer in Storage upgrades Fuel Production to Advanced (needs **Allow removals**). A **Panopticon Instance** or **Core** in Storage goes into your Surveillance Center. Neither counts for inspections.

## Random Assortment of Things

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26260.0). Founds your one Frontiers settlement once the best site would make 20,000 a month. Builds Refining and a Trading Post if they pay back in two years. Other facilities are left to you.

## Station Augments

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=30583.0). Picks the best augments for any mod's stations: output while calm, combat (counted 4x) under high threat or with a raid or blockade coming. A well-defended system stays on output at high threat. Your own installs are not changed. Refits take 7 days, or none with your fleet in the system.

## Stations from other mods

[Interstellar Imperium](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=8007.0) | [Knights of Ludd](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29124.0)

Their stations, Roider's and Ashes of the Domain's count like vanilla ones. With Interstellar Imperium you can pick the Imperium line.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=17094.0)

- Terraforming projects are valued and started, with prerequisites: [Terraforming](terraforming.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled).
- **Mining and siphon stations** are founded once researched, with colony-limit room, in calm or defended systems with your Auto colonies. A crew takes 60 days and TASC's costs plus a fee. The station runs on Auto with the Money goal.
- **TASC stations** (Settings, Advanced, New colonies): Auto stations only recommend while New colonies is on Auto, Money for new colonies is Money ladder and the Expansion rung is Recommend only or Off.
- Astropolis stations are left to you.

## Grand.Colonies

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=20986.0). Without it, nothing is built past 12 buildings. With it, upkeep past its limit counts in each build's cost.

## Substance.Abuse

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24378.0). A Brewery needs a recipe in Storage (or a Recipe Book the colony is big enough for) and no idle Brewery.

## Volkov Industrial Conglomerate

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=19603.0). Viral contamination treatment comes first. Soil nanites only go on vanilla farmland.

## Roider Union

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9547.0). Dives and the Union HQ are valued by nearby deposits and the Historical Roider Charter. Rockpiper Shipworks only after you buy its blueprint at a Union HQ.

## Planetary Shield: Access Control

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14990.0). The shield is valued as ground defense plus lower Pather interest. A colony with a working shield and Pather cells keeps its port closed.

## Unknown Skies, What We Left Behind, Astral Ascension

[Unknown Skies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29876.0) | [What We Left Behind](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=25205.0) | [Astral Ascension](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=28273.0)

Their mining conditions and water worlds count. Hazard pay does not push planetless colonies past size 10.

## Omega Core Everywhere and Astral Ascension Renewed

[Omega Core Everywhere](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36168.0) | [Astral Ascension Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36167.0)

- **Omega Core Everywhere:** an Omega core in Storage goes where it does the most, by that mod's table. It does not count for inspections. With the mod's building effects off in LunaLib, Omega cores stay out of buildings.
- **Astral Ascension Renewed:** an Omega core can govern a colony. It counts 10 inspection points, so it goes only where **Inspection risk** allows.
- Taking the core from a colony past size 10 starts that mod's Great Civil War. You are warned when an inspection heads for an AI core administrator.

## Niko's Scavengable Industries

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36195.0). Learned blueprints are built when they pay. **Left to you:** Blast-Core Mining, Spy Bureau and Outposts, Cryosanctum, Planetary Defense Network, Orbital Flak Cannons, Innovation Shipworks, the Artificial Shrine.

## Junk Yard Dogs and Carter's Freetraders

[Junk Yard Dogs](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22826.0) | [Carter's Freetraders](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24433.0)

Their forges and Biolab are built like anything else. Carter's drugs and hand weapons are illegal for your faction, so outside a free port they count only for local needs.

## Vulpoids

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29929.0)

- **Organ Farms** and the **Biotech Facility** upgrade are built when they pay.
- **Colony items:** Midas Nanoforge (not in a forge on a habitable world), Mangonut Mother Tree, Mutated Bioforge, Nanofiltration Core (only where it clears pollution), Lobster Bioforge (only in a Biotech Facility).
- **Left to you:** the Vulpoid Bioforge, vulpoid workforces, in-person Profecto work.

## Yunru Industries, Slightly Better Tech-Mining, Yunru's Worlds

[Yunru's mod thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=21968.0) (all three)

- **Secondary Drydocks:** valued by what it adds beside Heavy Industry or Orbital Works (best source only). No nanoforge.
- **Auxiliary Shipbays** and **Advent Outreach:** measured like anything else. Advent's story point improvement is skipped.
- The **Bazaar** only goes at your [capital](capital.md).
- **Slightly Better Tech-Mining** uses its own numbers. Yunru's world conditions are read from colony stats.

## United Aurora Federation

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=12709.0). Placed by effect: Dimensional Nanoforge, Servosync Pump, Modular Purifier, Garrison Transmitter. Not installed: Rice Cooker, Dimensional Stove, Access Router.

## Oddisz's mods

[Mod list](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36175.0). Omega mods: [above](#omega-core-everywhere-and-astral-ascension-renewed).

- **[Reverse Engineered Private Arsenal](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35627.0)** (also in Coalesced): left to you. Taking the Reverse Engineering Hub down deletes its markets.

- **[Sol Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35983.0):** industries in Sol are priced for Sol's crisis, which does not go down.
- **Checked, nothing needed:** [Gambling Den](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35993.0), [Extra System Reloaded - Oddisz Edition](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36143.0), [Hullmods - Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35619.0), [Intel Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35978.0) (hiding Colony Autopilot's intel does not stop it), [StopBloatingMe](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35850.0), [StopStackingMe](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35952.0), [StarterPack](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35856.0), [Discord Rich Presence](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35392.0).

## Auto Outfitting

[GitHub](https://github.com/Mjolniar/auto-outfitting). Its big weapon orders use Ashes of the Domain's production orders. Goods none of your colonies make get a producer planned ([Research](research.md#special-project-supply)).

## Any other mod
- Deposits in the game's tables and installable colony items are handled like vanilla ones.
- Mod buildings with no formula are measured on a throwaway test colony: [How it builds](how-it-builds.md#mod-buildings-it-doesnt-know).
- Anything unsupported is listed in Diagnostics and left alone.
