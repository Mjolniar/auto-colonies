# Low profile

## Quick version
- Low profile is an optional expansion style. It keeps Hegemony inspection risk, Pather interest and crisis progress low.
- The default style builds whatever pays most, uses AI cores everywhere and opens free ports. It earns more and triggers more crises.
- Set it under **Settings, Expansion style**. Colonies replan right away.
- Low profile earns less money.

## What it changes
- **Crisis cost.** Anything that raises crisis progress is valued at ten times the normal cost, so it has to earn much more to be built. Patrol HQ, Military Base and High Command slow every crisis, so they're worth ten times more and get built sooner.
- **Hegemony.** No AI cores above size 3. Cores already there go back to Autopilot Storage, one a day. An AI core administrator is swapped for a human once one is free.
- **Pather cells.** Nothing gets built, installed or slotted that takes a size 4+ colony to 7 Pather interest. Items holding it over go back to Storage, least useful first. Buildings are never torn down for this.
- **Tri-Tachyon.** One competing good is free. Past that, a building must be worth it.
- **Sindrian Diktat.** Any building making 4+ fuel is priced for the Diktat's interest.
- **Luddic Church.** Buildings that prevent a Luddic majority (Mining, Refining, Heavy Industry, Fuel Production, Military Base, High Command) get credit. Patrol HQ doesn't count.
- **Free ports are off,** since they draw Nexerelin's police.
- **Fights.** It doesn't raid other factions. Defense, relief and strikes on pirate and Pather bases still go. Agents don't sabotage and inspections aren't resisted.
- **Omega and IX cores** aren't used, and it doesn't settle next to a Remnant nexus.

## When it lets up
Each rule ends once that faction's crisis is over for good:

- **Hegemony:** beat three inspections, or join the Persean League.
- **Luddic Path:** beat their attack or pay the tithe.
- **Tri-Tachyon:** raid their commerce.
- **Diktat:** beat their attack or take their deal.
- **Luddic Church:** beat the Knights or take the deal.

The Crises page shows which rules are still on.

## What it can't avoid
- **Pirates.** They grow with your biggest colony. Military goes up sooner, and beating one big raid ends pirate crises for good.
- **The Persean League.** At a size 5 colony it wants you to join and blockades you until you do. Joining at Kazeron also ends the Hegemony crisis. Joining is left to you.
