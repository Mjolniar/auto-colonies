# Supported mods

## Quick version

- None of these are required, and support kicks on when the mod's enabled.
- Diagnostics (empire panel) shows the status for each mod.
- This only applies to Auto colonies ([Modes](how-it-builds.md#modes)). Let me know on [Issues](https://github.com/Mjolniar/auto-colonies/issues) if something breaks.

## Mods

- [Terraforming Made Easy](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29441.0) is covered in [Terraforming](terraforming.md). It also uses a Beta core for relay, buoy and array upgrades ([Stable locations](how-it-builds.md#stable-locations)).
- With [Industrial Evolution](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=18011.0), it handles ambassadors (30 days), fitting edicts, VPC chips, the mining and refining specialty and a Courier Port for shortages. Harsh edicts aren't automated.
- [Nexerelin](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9175.0) adds threat, sieges, the police crisis and the Derelict Empire start ([War and security](war-and-security.md), [Money](money.md#autonomy-nexerelin)). A [New colonies](expansion.md) expedition costs 165,000 (100 points) or 205,000 (150).
- With [Ashes of the Domain, VoK, ToT](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26307.0) and [Building Menu Overhaul](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=31308.0), locked buildings get skipped, buildings are scored by effect, trade contracts go at 15% spare and research works as in [Research](research.md). Grand Wonders except the Daedalus Array, Star Citadels and custom production are left to you.
- With [Emergent Threats (IX Revival)](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=27284.0), an Antimatter Stabilizer upgrades Fuel Production and the Panopticon goes into the Surveillance Center.
- With [Random Assortment of Things](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26260.0), it starts a Frontiers settlement at a 20,000 a month site, and Refining and Trading Post go in at a 2-year payback. Forge-Assisted Industry BPs in Autopilot Storage go to Expedition Hub and Advanced Asteroid Mining. The Expedition Hub is valued by the uninhabited planets its fleets can reach from each colony, so the BP goes where it brings back the most, and it gets an AI core when the extra 10 light-years are worth it.
- With [Station Augments](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=30583.0), it picks output augments while calm and combat ones under high threat, and a refit takes 7 days.
- With [Interstellar Imperium](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=8007.0) and [Knights of Ludd](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29124.0), stations count like vanilla.
- Without [Grand.Colonies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=20986.0), it plans for 12 buildings maximum.
- With [Substance.Abuse](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24378.0), the Brewery needs a recipe in Storage.
- With [Volkov Industrial Conglomerate](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=19603.0), viral contamination treatment comes first, and soil nanites go on vanilla farmland.
- With [Roider Union](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9547.0), Dives and Union HQ are valued by deposits, and Rockpiper Shipworks waits for its blueprint.
- [Planetary Shield: Access Control](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14990.0) is valued as ground defense plus lower Pather interest.
- With [Unknown Skies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29876.0), [What We Left Behind](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=25205.0) and [Astral Ascension](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=28273.0), mining conditions and water worlds count.
- With [Niko's Scavengable Industries](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36195.0), learned blueprints get built when they pay.
- With [Junk Yard Dogs](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22826.0) and [Carter's Freetraders](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24433.0), forges and the Biolab get built normally.
- With [Vulpoids](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29929.0), Organ Farms and Biotech get upgraded when they pay, and its colony items are placed by effect.
- With [Yunru Industries, Slightly Better Tech-Mining, Yunru's Worlds](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=21968.0), buildings are valued by effect, and the Bazaar only goes at your [capital](capital.md).
- With [United Aurora Federation](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=12709.0), the Dimensional Nanoforge, Servosync Pump, Modular Purifier and Garrison Transmitter are placed by effect.
- With [Auto Outfitting](https://github.com/Mjolniar/auto-outfitting), big weapon orders go through AotD production orders.
- With [Kadur Remnant](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35145.0), Kadur Majority Faith's +1 output on industrial buildings (size 3 and up) and Virus Bombed's -2 on every output line count in build values.
- With [JaydeePiracy](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36201.0), Quarry Lakes (Refining needs no ore), Domain-era Meshugatron (Fuel Production +3 fuel) and Eutrophicated Biosphere (Farming adds organics) count in build values. The Planetkiller Launch Site and the planetkiller item are left to you.
- With [Corrupt.Officials](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35125.0), extra income at a colony above the cutoff counts at the share the colony keeps, with settings read from LunaLib.
- With [Tahlan Shipworks](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14935.0), it won't found a new colony on a Kassadari Claim planet.
- With [Gladiator Society](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=33586.0), it won't found a new colony on The Gulf.
- With [Scy Nation](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29535.0), [CJY's Toy Box](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35093.0) and [AI-Retrofits](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22261.0), the Intelligence Command Center, Private Production Facility, both greenhouses and Population Replacement Center are left to you.
- With [Blue](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14041.0) and [Oculian Armada](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9035.0), Lanestate Battleyards and the Orbital Matrix count as ship producers for the production floors.
- With [Cloning](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35130.0) and [Coalesced Little Mods](https://github.com/andrzejsokolowski/starsector-coalesced-little-mods), Clone Vat Experiments, Cloning Industry and the Megafactory get built when they're worth it.
- Anything from [Reverse Engineered Private Arsenal](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35627.0) is left to you.
- With [Toolbox Economy Curves](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36178.0), it plans with the curve you picked.
- With [Sol Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35983.0), industries are priced for Sol's crisis.
- With [Secrets of the Frontier](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=15820.0), its habitat objective is left to you, and Station Augments' Warmind Refit gets skipped while the Dustkeepers are hostile.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=17094.0)

- Terraforming projects get valued and started ([Terraforming](terraforming.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled)).
- Mining and siphon stations get founded once researched, with room under the colony limit. They take 60 days and cost TASC's price plus a fee.
- TASC stations are only recommended while New colonies is Auto and Expansion is Recommend only or Off.

## Omega Core Everywhere and Astral Ascension Renewed

[Omega Core Everywhere](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36168.0) | [Astral Ascension Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36167.0)

- With Omega Core Everywhere, an Omega core in Storage goes where it does the most, with no inspection points.
- With Astral Ascension Renewed, an Omega core can govern a colony (10 inspection points).

## Rogue Planets

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36337.0)

- The synergy pairs Degenerate Volatiles, Diamond Core, Radiolytic Catalyst, Hollowed Caldera Garrisons, Vacation Paradise & Resort, Smugglers' Haven, Viable Slave Labor, Magmatic Antimatter Forge and Ground-to-Space Missile Defense count in build values.
- Hydrocarbon Fractionation, Megalithic Outcrops, Clandestine Harvesting Syndicate and Pilgrim World count in build values too, and upkeep conditions count in every upkeep.
- On a Fragile Biosphere world, Farming's the only industry it builds or plans.
- With a Volatile Additive Reactor, the second of Refining and Fuel Production is left to you, and a running pair isn't taken down, replaced or upgraded.
- Viable Slave Labor and Supercooled Cryo-Sinks colonies are held at 6 stability or more. In the second month of slave labor unrest, Commerce gets taken down if that lifts stability above 5.
- A Pilgrim World gets no AI cores or AI administrator.
- Hazard pay stays off while Diamond Core runs, and the free port costs no stability while Smugglers' Haven runs.
- For the production floors, Degenerate Volatiles, Ship Graveyard and Magmatic Antimatter Forge colonies rank first as floor hosts, and the Magmatic forge counts at the production gathering point.
- When it looks for new colonies, Antimatter Anomaly fuel and crustquake goods count, and Fragile Biosphere only counts farmland.
- It leaves Forgotten Vault-World's Tech-Mining, the Trash King's seat, the Violet Dwarf system and the Afterlight to you. Cold Sky Observatory and Resonant Signal Trap get no build value.
- On the colony panel, Mod features lists each condition at the colony and how Autopilot handles it.

## Any other mod

- Deposits and colony items get handled like vanilla. Buildings with no formula get measured on a test colony ([How it builds](how-it-builds.md#mod-buildings)). Other than that, it leaves unsupported mods alone.
