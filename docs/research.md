# Research

Needs Ashes of the Domain (Vaults of Knowledge). Settings, Advanced: **Extras** (Research, Special project supply, Megastructures), **AI cores and items** (Alphas in research buildings, Research uses colony items), **Surplus and money ladder** (Surplus research, Wonders and gates).

## Quick version

- **Research** on **Auto** (recommended; also Advisor, Off) starts the best tech when nothing is running or queued. Your own queue is left alone.
- Techs are valued by what they unlock at your colonies, plan techs first.

## How it picks research

- **Value.** What it unlocks at your colonies, its own effects, and half the best tech it leads to. Techs in a locked plan step's chain share its value.
- **Research Facility.** With none, one goes first in the focus world's plan.
- **Sophia Anderson** is made head of R&D first (one databank off every tech).
- **Databanks** go only to plan techs while the best plan tech is short. After 90 days, the best affordable tech paying back within a year goes anyway.
- **Fillers** (no plan needs them, no payback within a year) start only while spending freely or from Idle money up, and not while a plan tech waits for databanks.
- **Shipping.** Missing inputs come from the nearest colony, for a fee and a couple of days.
- **Research page**: upkeep with your facilities against one (2: 1.82x, 4: 3.08x, 8: 4.71x).

## Techs that take colony items

Yours to start by default. **Research uses colony items** lets Auto take them while credits are over the surplus line and the tech has everything else: **From storage** (Autopilot Storage and Storage tabs) or **From storage and buildings** (where the item does least first; locked buildings skipped).

## AI cores in research buildings

A core cuts the building's own costs: Alpha half, Beta a quarter, Gamma a tenth. **Alphas in research buildings** on **First** (recommended) puts Alphas there first.

## Surplus research

Extra Research Facilities at Auto colonies, one at a time. Each past the first adds +10% research speed, up to 8 facilities.

- Starts from the money tier Surplus up, with the Economy rung On ([Money](money.md#economy)).
- The extra speed must be worth more than the upkeep (from Idle money: half of it).
- Below Rich, at most 3 of its own. The endgame builds them while research is left.

## Special project supply

Black site projects and open production orders take goods from your colonies. No faction makes Electronic Components, Refined Metal or Enriched Transplutonics.

- **Auto** (recommended): a needed good none of your colonies make gets a producer planned as a first step at the best colony.
- **Warn only** tells you. **Off** does nothing.

## Wonders and gates

Builds the Daedalus Array and Bifrost network once your colonies make every good each needs. Rules: [Money](money.md#wonders).
