# Your capital

Every empire needs a home port. The capital is one colony where all your stuff ends up and where you come back to sell what you found, buy what you need and refit.

## Which colony

By default the manager picks it: the colony where your things already are. Your production gathering point counts most, then whatever's sitting in its Storage tab (ships count a lot), then size and accessibility. Once it's picked it stays put, since moving home is a big deal.

Want a different one? Hit **Make this the capital** on that colony's panel, pick it under **Settings, Capital**, or just move your production gathering point to it in the game's own custom production screen. The capital follows you.

## Everything goes there

- **Custom production.** The production gathering point follows the capital, so ships, weapons and fighters you order show up there.
- **Your other Storage tabs.** Once a week, ships, weapons, fighter wings, blueprints and hull mod specs sitting in your other Storage tabs (colonies, plus any market where you rent storage) get shipped to the capital's Storage. Supplies, fuel, crew and marines go too. That's storage logistics doing it, so it costs the same: what an Autopilot Storage shipment costs plus 1,000 a ship, and it takes 2 days plus a day a light-year. Turn it off with **Gather your Storage there** if you'd rather keep things spread out.
- Colony items and AI cores stay in Autopilot Storage like always. Those go where they do the most good, not home.

If you've set your own refit hub or sell hub under Storage logistics, those still win.

## Making it a place to trade

Your first colony's job is making money, so the fast buildout and the early game are left alone. Once things ramp up (no fast buildout running at the capital and last month's net income over 100,000 by default), the capital starts building itself into a trade hub. These go to the front of its plan:

1. **Spaceport**, then **Megaport**. Traders need somewhere to dock, and most of the buildings below need a working spaceport.
2. **Commerce** - gives your colony an Open Market you can buy and sell at, plus 25% income. That's the big one; in the base game it's the only way to get a market on your own colony. (Industrial Evolution allows one per star system, and Ashes of the Domain locks it behind research. Its Trade Outpost upgrades into it.)
3. **Trade Outpost** (Ashes of the Domain) - a no-tariff market for legal goods, open the first 7 days of each month.
4. **Bazaar** (Yunru Industries) - another Open Market, and a black market while the colony's a free port.
5. **Requisitions Centre** (Industrial Evolution) - weapons to buy from factions that like you.
6. **Union HQ** (Roider Union) - the Roiders Market, with military hardware, and it buys stuff that's illegal elsewhere. Needs the blueprint; Roider Dives come first.
7. **Salvage Yards** (Industrial Evolution) - order ships built from scrap, and scrap hulls you don't want.
8. **Underworld** (Ashes of the Domain) - a black market. Needs research and a free port.
9. **Waystation** - fuel and supplies stockpiled at home.

Anything from a mod you don't have just gets skipped, and so does anything that can't be built at that colony (research it doesn't have, a blueprint you don't know, one already in the system). Military bases and free ports don't give your own colony a market in this game, so they aren't on the list, and Interstellar Imperium's Bazaar can't be built by players.

The capital also gets its station sooner than other colonies: a Battlestation from size 5 and a Star Fortress from size 7 (see [How it builds](how-it-builds.md)).

Like any plan step they reserve industry slots ahead of other industries, and they get built even if they cost more than they earn, since the point is having a place to trade at home.

If every industry slot is already taken, the capital takes down its weakest ordinary industry to make room, at most one every 90 days. It never touches defenses, fleet buildings, anything that builds ships, mod buildings it can't value for sure, or buildings another plan needs (research, special projects, terraforming worlds). The building's core and item go to Autopilot Storage. Turn that off with **Make room for trade buildings** if you'd rather free slots yourself.

If your net income drops below zero, the trade build-up waits until you're back in the black.

## Settings

All under **Settings, Capital**:

- **Capital** - Autopilot picks (recommended), or one of your colonies.
- **Capital does** - Deliveries and commerce (recommended), Deliveries only, or Off.
- **Commerce starts** - when net income passes 100,000 a month (recommended), 50,000, 250,000, or right away.
- **Make room for trade buildings** - Yes (recommended) or No.
- **Gather your Storage there** - Yes (recommended) or No.
