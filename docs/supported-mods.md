# Supported mods

None of these are required. Each one turns on by itself when its mod is enabled, and any mix of them works. The empire panel's **Diagnostics** page shows how each one is doing (OK, newer version, partly off, off).

Unless it says otherwise, the manager only acts on colonies in Auto. Approve colonies get a proposal and Advisor colonies a recommendation, and everything follows your budget.

If a mod you use isn't here and you'd like it supported, throw it in the [issues](https://github.com/Mjolniar/jonahs-colony-manager/issues).

## Terraforming Made Easy

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29441.0)

Special-world plans (Forge, Fortress, Paradise, Hive, Death, Waste) built from TME's own requirement table, the plan's terraforming, and surplus terraforming at every Auto colony. All in [Terraforming](terraforming.md).

## Industrial Evolution

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=18011.0)

- **Ambassadors** - hires one for an empty Embassy and installs one waiting in a colony's normal Storage, with a focus (worst relations first and so on), a priority per faction, and a level where it stops. One hired from afar takes 30 days to arrive.
- **Senate edicts** - enacts the one that suits the colony. The harsh ones (forced labour, cleansing, single-child, labour limits) are never automated.
- **VPC chips** - installs chips from Autopilot Storage.
- **Specialized Mining and Refining** - switches to whichever specialty is worth the most.
- **Supply shipping** - when a colony runs short, it sets up a monthly Courier Port contract from another colony's stockpile and cancels it once the shortage is gone. Only the best-connected colony gets a Courier Port.
- Its campaign consumables aren't allowed in Autopilot Storage.

## Nexerelin

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9175.0)

Emergency defense fleets, war fleets (relief, defense, pirate base strikes, raids on whoever attacked you, never invasions), agents, the threat estimate, sieges, the police crisis, and the Derelict Empire start. All in [War and security](war-and-security.md). Autonomy is in [Money](money.md#autonomy-nexerelin). Nexerelin's player outposts are left alone completely, since they sit outside your economy.

## Ashes of the Domain

[Ashes of the Domain (Vaults of Knowledge, Theory of Toolbox)](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26307.0) | [Building Menu Overhaul](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=31308.0)

Covers Building Menu Overhaul, Vaults of Knowledge and Theory of Toolbox.

- **Research locks and upgrade paths** - it asks Building Menu Overhaul the same questions the build menu does, so nothing locked or hidden ever gets built, and AotD's upgrades (Monoculture Plots to Farming and so on) are started the way the menu does it.
- **Starter buildings** and **industry synergies** are scored, and the colony panel shows active synergies and ones a building away.
- **Management styles** - new buildings are scored with the style's real effects. The style itself is never changed. On a Floating City nothing is built while fuel would run short.
- **Research**, **special project supply**, **wonders and gates** - see [Research](research.md).
- **Trade contracts** - takes an offer only when ToT's own numbers leave 15% to spare and it pays more than exporting would.
- **Megastructures** - the Coronal Hypershunt (wormhole range kept to what reaches your receivers, the collector and wormhole restored when you can afford it), Pluto Mining Station and the Nidavelir Complex. Anything you change yourself is left alone until you click **Let Autopilot manage it**.
- **Colony items** - AotD's items are placed like vanilla ones.
- **Left to you**: Grand Wonders other than the Daedalus Array, Star Citadels, custom production, and picking a management style.

## Emergent Threats (IX Revival)

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=27284.0)

With an Antimatter Stabilizer in Autopilot Storage, it upgrades Fuel Production to Advanced Fuel Production the way the mod's portmaster does, keeping the core, item and improvement. This replaces a building, so it needs **Allow removals** on.

## Random Assortment of Things

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26260.0)

Founds your one Frontiers settlement on the best surveyed site once it would make at least 20,000 a month (by sending an expedition, which gets there 10 days plus a day a light-year after you pay; it's refunded if the site's gone by then), then builds Refining and a Trading Post there when they pay back within two years. The other facilities change how you play, so they're yours. Its Forge-Assisted Industry BP isn't allowed in Autopilot Storage.

## Station Augments

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=30583.0)

Picks the best set of augments each station can fit, scored by what each one actually does on that station (its own guns, fighters, shields and AI core). Output augments while things are calm, combat when the colony is threatened. Augments you installed yourself are never changed. Station Augments only lets you change them while docked, so from afar a refit crew makes the change 7 days later (at once while your fleet is in the system). Works on any mod's stations.

## Stations from other mods

[Interstellar Imperium](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=8007.0) | [Knights of Ludd](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29124.0)

Interstellar Imperium, Knights of Ludd and Ashes of the Domain stations count like vanilla ones everywhere (defense, cores, the plan's station step). With Interstellar Imperium you can pick the Imperium station line.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=17094.0)

Its terraforming projects are valued and started, with the prerequisite buildings built when needed. Some of its buildings are scored from estimates. See [Terraforming](terraforming.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled).

## Grand.Colonies

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=20986.0)

Without it nothing is built past 12 buildings, since the colony screen can't show a 13th. With it, the extra upkeep past its limit is counted in each build's cost.

## Substance.Abuse

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24378.0)

A Brewery is built only when Autopilot Storage holds a recipe (or a Recipe Book the colony is big enough for) and no Brewery is idle, then the recipe goes in.

## Volkov Industrial Conglomerate

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=19603.0)

Viral contamination treatment comes first, tier by tier, to stop a colony shrinking. Soil nanites only go where the farmland is vanilla, since other farmland crashes them.

## Roider Union

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9547.0)

Dives and the Union HQ are valued by the deposits they can mine nearby, with the Historical Roider Charter's extra output and range counted when one's in. Heads up: Roider takes a heavy machinery shortage off every good they mine, so being 1 short can zero out a Dives, and that's counted too. Its Charter gets placed like any colony item. Rockpiper Shipworks only shows up once you've bought its blueprint at a Union HQ, and then it's valued like any other building. Roider stations count like vanilla ones.

## Planetary Shield: Access Control

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14990.0)

The shield is valued as ground defense plus lower Pather interest, and a colony with a working shield and Pather cells keeps its port closed, since that's how the shield works.

## Unknown Skies, What We Left Behind, Astral Ascension

[Unknown Skies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29876.0) | [What We Left Behind](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=25205.0) | [Astral Ascension](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=28273.0)

Their extra mining conditions count toward Mining, their water worlds count as water worlds, and planetless colonies (the original Astral Ascension) never get pushed past size 10 by hazard pay, since they crash there.

## Omega Core Everywhere and Astral Ascension Renewed

[Omega Core Everywhere](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36168.0) | [Astral Ascension Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36167.0)

Drop Omega cores into Autopilot Storage and the manager finds them a home. Without one of these two mods an Omega core barely does anything in a colony, so storage won't take them.

- **Omega Core Everywhere**: an Omega core goes into whichever building it does the most in, going by that mod's own table. Doubled output from half the inputs (at double upkeep) in production buildings, doubled income in Commerce and the like, doubled fleets in military buildings, and so on. A core in a building doesn't count toward Hegemony inspections, so it fits any colony. If you switch the mod's building effects off in LunaLib, the manager leaves Omega cores out of buildings.
- **Astral Ascension Renewed**: an Omega core can govern a colony instead, which is huge (every building makes twice as much, +3 stability, an extra industry slot, and it's the only way past size 10). It counts 10 Hegemony inspection points as an administrator though, so it only goes where your **Inspection risk** setting allows that. Otherwise you get a log line and it's your call.
- Once a colony governed by an Omega core is past size 10, taking the core away starts that mod's Great Civil War. The manager never touches an AI core administrator, and it warns you when an inspection is heading for one.

## Niko's Scavengable Industries

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36195.0)

Its buildings need their blueprint learned first (right click it in your cargo), and the manager only ever builds what you know. A lot of them do things you'd want to decide yourself, so these are **left to you** and never built automatically: Blast-Core Mining (it eats your deposits over time), the Spy Bureau and Spy Outposts (they go after other factions), the Cryosanctum (starts the Nomios crisis), the Planetary Defense Network (caps the colony at size 5), the Orbital Flak Cannons, the Innovation Shipworks and the Artificial Shrine. The rest (Training Grounds and so on) get measured and built when they pay.

## Junk Yard Dogs and Carter's Freetraders

[Junk Yard Dogs](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22826.0) | [Carter's Freetraders](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24433.0)

Their resource forges and Biolab get measured and built like anything else. Heads up on Carter's: the drugs and hand weapons it makes are illegal for your faction, so unless the colony is a free port they don't export, and the manager only counts them for what that colony needs itself.

## Vulpoids

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29929.0)

- **Organ Farms** and the **Biotech Facility** upgrade get built when they pay.
- Its colony items get placed: the Midas Nanoforge (like a pristine nanoforge, but never in a forge on a habitable world, since it pollutes like vanilla's), the Mangonut Mother Tree, the Mutated Bioforge, the Nanofiltration Core (only where it actually clears pollution, bad weather or toxic air and the colony has the volatiles and transplutonics it eats), and the Lobster Bioforge (only in a Biotech Facility, since it stops an Organ Farm's organs).
- **Profecto Vulpoid administrators** you've hired get placed like any other administrator, with their own skill counted.
- **Left to you**: the Vulpoid Bioforge itself (it kicks off the whole vulpoid acceptance story, with sanctions and the Knights of Ludd coming for that colony), vulpoid workforces, and anything you'd do in person with the Profectos.

## Yunru Industries, Slightly Better Tech-Mining, Yunru's Worlds

[Yunru's mod thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=21968.0) (all three)

- **Secondary Drydocks** is valued by what it adds next to your Heavy Industry or Orbital Works (it doesn't stack with them, a colony only counts its best source) or as a Heavy Industry that never pollutes on its own. It can't take a nanoforge.
- **Auxiliary Shipbays** and **Advent Outreach** get measured like anything else. Advent's story point improvement does nothing, so it's never improved.
- The **Bazaar** is a market for you to trade at and does nothing for the colony, so it's left to you.
- **Slightly Better Tech-Mining** makes Tech-Mining a lot richer, and the manager values it with that mod's numbers instead of vanilla's.
- Yunru's world conditions are read through the colony's own stats.

## United Aurora Federation

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=12709.0)

Its colony items are placed from what they actually do: the Dimensional Nanoforge, Servosync Pump, Modular Purifier and Garrison Transmitter. The Rice Cooker, Dimensional Stove and Access Router are never installed, since they cost more than they add or do nothing.

## Jonah's Auto Outfitting

[GitHub](https://github.com/Mjolniar/jonahs-auto-outfitting)

Its big weapon orders for a fleet go through Ashes of the Domain's production orders, which take goods from your colonies as they go. Any good those orders need that none of your colonies make gets a producer planned, same as for special projects. See [Research](research.md#special-project-supply).

## Any other mod

- Deposits a mod registers in the game's own tables are scored like vanilla ones.
- Any mod's colony item the game knows how to install gets placed from Autopilot Storage.
- Mod buildings with no formula are measured on a throwaway test colony and scored from what they really do. See [How it builds](how-it-builds.md#mod-buildings-it-doesnt-know).
- Anything it can't support is listed in Diagnostics and left alone.
