# Supported mods

## Quick version
- None of these mods are required. Support for each one turns on when that mod is enabled. Any combination works.
- The empire panel's **Diagnostics** page shows the status of each one: OK, newer version, partly off, off.
- Only Auto colonies are acted on unless noted. Approve colonies get a proposal and Advisor colonies a recommendation. Everything is paid from the budget.
- To request support for a mod, open an [issue](https://github.com/Mjolniar/jonahs-colony-manager/issues).

## Terraforming Made Easy

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29441.0)

Special-world plans, plan terraforming and surplus terraforming. See [Terraforming](terraforming.md). Its comm relay, nav buoy and sensor array upgrade is used with a spare Beta core (see [Stable locations](how-it-builds.md#stable-locations)).

## Industrial Evolution

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=18011.0)

- **Ambassadors:** hires one for an empty Embassy, or installs one from a colony's Storage. Takes 30 days to arrive.
- **Senate edicts:** enacts the one that suits the colony. Harsh ones (forced labour, cleansing, single-child, labour limits) are never automated.
- **VPC chips:** installed from Autopilot Storage.
- **Specialized Mining and Refining:** picks the best specialty.
- **Supply shipping:** a monthly Courier Port contract covers a shortage, then cancels. Only the best-connected colony gets a Courier Port.
- Its campaign consumables aren't allowed in Autopilot Storage.

## Nexerelin

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9175.0)

Defense fleets, war fleets, agents, threat, sieges, the police crisis and the Derelict Empire start. See [War and security](war-and-security.md). Autonomy is in [Money](money.md#autonomy-nexerelin). Player outposts are left alone.

## Ashes of the Domain

[Ashes of the Domain (Vaults of Knowledge, Theory of Toolbox)](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26307.0) | [Building Menu Overhaul](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=31308.0)

Covers Building Menu Overhaul, Vaults of Knowledge and Theory of Toolbox.

- **Locks and upgrades:** nothing locked or hidden gets built, and AotD upgrades start the way the menu does it.
- **Starter buildings and synergies** are scored. The colony panel shows active ones and ones a building away.
- **Management styles** are scored with their real effects and never changed. A Floating City builds nothing while fuel would run short.
- **Research, special project supply, wonders:** see [Research](research.md).
- **Trade contracts:** only when ToT's numbers leave 15% spare and it beats exporting.
- **Hypershunt Receivers** go at stable spots once researched, with a Coronal Hypershunt Tap in Storage (see [Stable locations](how-it-builds.md#stable-locations)).
- **Megastructures:** Coronal Hypershunt, Pluto Mining Station, Nidavelir Complex. Anything you change yourself waits until you click **Let Autopilot manage it**.
- **Colony items** are placed like vanilla ones.
- **Left to you:** Grand Wonders other than the Daedalus Array, Star Citadels, custom production, management style.

## Emergent Threats (IX Revival)

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=27284.0)

With an Antimatter Stabilizer in Storage, Fuel Production upgrades to Advanced (needs **Allow removals**). A **Panopticon Instance** or **Core** in Storage goes into your Surveillance Center. Neither counts for Hegemony inspections.

## Random Assortment of Things

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26260.0)

Founds your one Frontiers settlement once the best site would make 20,000 a month (expedition, refunded if the site's gone), then builds Refining and a Trading Post if they pay back in two years. Other facilities are yours. Its Forge-Assisted Industry BP isn't allowed in Storage.

## Station Augments

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=30583.0)

Picks the best augments each station can fit, on any mod's stations. Output while calm, combat when threatened. Your own installs are never changed. From afar a refit crew takes 7 days (instant while your fleet is in the system).

## Stations from other mods

[Interstellar Imperium](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=8007.0) | [Knights of Ludd](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29124.0)

Their stations and Ashes of the Domain's count like vanilla ones. With Interstellar Imperium you can pick the Imperium line. A station in a well-defended system stays economic even at high threat, unless a raid or blockade is coming.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=17094.0)

Terraforming projects are valued and started, with prerequisites. See [Terraforming](terraforming.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled). **Mining stations** and **siphon stations** can be founded as colonies in calm or defended systems with your Auto colonies, once researched and with colony-limit room. A crew does it for TASC's costs plus a fee and 60 days, and the colony runs in Auto with the Money goal. **Station construction** starts on Recommend only. Switch it to Auto to build them. Astropolis stations are left to you.

## Grand.Colonies

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=20986.0)

Without it, nothing is built past 12 buildings. With it, upkeep past its limit is counted in each build's cost.

## Substance.Abuse

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24378.0)

A Brewery is built only when Storage holds a recipe (or a Recipe Book the colony is big enough for) and no Brewery is idle.

## Volkov Industrial Conglomerate

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=19603.0)

Viral contamination treatment comes first. Soil nanites only go on vanilla farmland.

## Roider Union

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9547.0)

Dives and the Union HQ are valued by nearby deposits, with the Historical Roider Charter counted. Roider takes a heavy machinery shortage off every good, and that's counted. Rockpiper Shipworks appears once you've bought its blueprint at a Union HQ. Roider stations count like vanilla ones.

## Planetary Shield: Access Control

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14990.0)

The shield is valued as ground defense plus lower Pather interest. A colony with a working shield and Pather cells keeps its port closed.

## Unknown Skies, What We Left Behind, Astral Ascension

[Unknown Skies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29876.0) | [What We Left Behind](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=25205.0) | [Astral Ascension](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=28273.0)

Their mining conditions and water worlds count. Planetless colonies are never pushed past size 10 by hazard pay.

## Omega Core Everywhere and Astral Ascension Renewed

[Omega Core Everywhere](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36168.0) | [Astral Ascension Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36167.0)

Drop Omega cores into Storage and the manager finds a home. Without one of these mods, storage won't take them.

- **Omega Core Everywhere:** the core goes where it does the most, by that mod's table. It doesn't count for Hegemony inspections. If you switch the mod's building effects off in LunaLib, Omega cores stay out of buildings.
- **Astral Ascension Renewed:** an Omega core can govern a colony (twice the output, +3 stability, an extra industry slot, the only way past size 10). It counts 10 inspection points, so it only goes where your **Inspection risk** setting allows. Otherwise you get a log line.
- Taking the core from a colony past size 10 starts that mod's Great Civil War. The manager never touches an AI core administrator and warns you when an inspection is heading for one.

## Niko's Scavengable Industries

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36195.0)

It only builds blueprints you've learned. **Left to you:** Blast-Core Mining, Spy Bureau and Outposts, Cryosanctum, Planetary Defense Network, Orbital Flak Cannons, Innovation Shipworks and the Artificial Shrine. The rest are built when they pay.

## Junk Yard Dogs and Carter's Freetraders

[Junk Yard Dogs](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22826.0) | [Carter's Freetraders](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24433.0)

Their forges and Biolab are built like anything else. Carter's drugs and hand weapons are illegal for your faction and don't export unless the colony is a free port, so they only count for the colony's own needs.

## Vulpoids

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29929.0)

- **Organ Farms** and the **Biotech Facility** upgrade are built when they pay.
- **Colony items** are placed: Midas Nanoforge (never in a forge on a habitable world), Mangonut Mother Tree, Mutated Bioforge, Nanofiltration Core (only where it clears pollution), Lobster Bioforge (only in a Biotech Facility).
- **Profecto administrators** you hired are placed like any other.
- **Left to you:** the Vulpoid Bioforge (it starts the acceptance story), vulpoid workforces, and in-person Profecto work.

## Yunru Industries, Slightly Better Tech-Mining, Yunru's Worlds

[Yunru's mod thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=21968.0) (all three)

- **Secondary Drydocks** is valued by what it adds beside Heavy Industry or Orbital Works (best source only). It can't take a nanoforge.
- **Auxiliary Shipbays** and **Advent Outreach** are measured like anything else. Advent's story point improvement does nothing, so it's skipped.
- The **Bazaar** does nothing for the colony, so it only goes at your [capital](capital.md).
- **Slightly Better Tech-Mining** is valued with that mod's own numbers.
- Yunru's world conditions are read through the colony's own stats.

## United Aurora Federation

[Forum thread](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=12709.0)

Placed by what they do: Dimensional Nanoforge, Servosync Pump, Modular Purifier, Garrison Transmitter. Never installed: Rice Cooker, Dimensional Stove, Access Router.

## Oddisz's mods

[Oddisz's mod list](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36175.0) (Omega Core Everywhere and Astral Ascension Renewed are covered [above](#omega-core-everywhere-and-astral-ascension-renewed))

- **[Cloning](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35130.0):** Clone Vat Experiments, the Cloning Industry and the Megafactory are built when the growth is worth it, with the mod's numbers and your LunaLib settings. Each costs stability that grows with size, so they only go where the penalty has room and never at a colony at its size limit.
- **[Reverse Engineered Private Arsenal](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35627.0):** left to you. Taking the Reverse Engineering Hub down deletes its markets, so it's never built, upgraded, taken down or emptied.
- **[Toolbox Economy Curves](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36178.0):** nothing needed. Whatever curve you pick is what it plans with.
- **[Sol Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35983.0):** Sol's crisis grows with every colony and industry there and never goes down. Industries in Sol are priced for it. The Crises page shows the meter.
- **[Coalesced Little Mods](https://github.com/andrzejsokolowski/starsector-coalesced-little-mods):** same buildings as Cloning and the Private Arsenal, same handling.
- **Checked, nothing needed:** [Gambling Den](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35993.0), [Extra System Reloaded - Oddisz Edition](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36143.0), [Hullmods - Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35619.0), [Intel Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35978.0) (hide the Colony Autopilot intel there and it keeps running), [StopBloatingMe](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35850.0), [StopStackingMe](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35952.0), [StarterPack](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35856.0) and [Discord Rich Presence](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35392.0).

## Jonah's Auto Outfitting

[GitHub](https://github.com/Mjolniar/jonahs-auto-outfitting)

Its big weapon orders go through Ashes of the Domain's production orders. Goods none of your colonies make get a producer planned. See [Research](research.md#special-project-supply).

## Any other mod
- Deposits registered in the game's tables are scored like vanilla ones.
- Any colony item the game can install gets placed from Storage.
- Mod buildings with no formula are measured on a throwaway test colony. See [How it builds](how-it-builds.md#mod-buildings-it-doesnt-know).
- Anything unsupported is listed in Diagnostics and left alone.
