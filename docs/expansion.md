# New colonies (Auto-expansion)

## Turning it on

- New colonies (Settings, Playstyle): Off (default), Ask first or Auto. Targets are fully surveyed, unclaimed planets.
- Money ladder (default for Money for new colonies): Auto asks first while the Expansion rung is Recommend only (default). A fixed line launches without asking.
- Expansion page: Proposal, expeditions, best 5 planets. Buttons: Found now, Never this planet, Clear.

## Where it looks

- Where (Settings, Advanced, New colonies): Systems you hold (default). Those, then within 6 light-years of a colony. Or within 15 light-years of the capital.
- Skipped: Remnant nexus systems, systems another faction claims or holds, unexplored ruins, mission sites, the RAT Frontiers settlement, planets already targeted, your never list.
- Ranking: Value a month at size 5, times location (same system x1.3, nearby x1.0, farther x0.7), less 2% per light-year from the capital.
- Minimum: 10,000 a month and a 60-month payback. At Rich, any positive value.

## Money and pace

- Money ladder (default): Needs Idle money and pays from the [Expansion rung](money.md#the-money-ladder).
- Fixed line (over 5,000,000, 25,000,000 or 50,000,000): An expedition may use 10% of the credits over the surplus line.
- Expeditions: One at a time (two at Endgame), 120 days apart at Idle money, 60 at Rich. The last colony must have finished its fast buildout or be 120 days old. None start during a siege, before a colony crisis, or with a Nexerelin invasion coming.
- Most colonies: Colony limit and administrators (default), or 5, 10, 15 or 20.
- Autopilot crew: 85,000 of founding goods at base price, plus 40,000 and 2,000 a light-year. 15 days plus travel.

## The new colony

- Mode and goal: Auto. The goal fits its plan (default) or one you pick. Past the colony limit, an administrator is hired at launch.
