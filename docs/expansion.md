# New colonies (Auto-expansion)

- New colonies (Settings, Playstyle) can be Off (default), Ask first or Auto. It only goes after fully surveyed, unclaimed planets, and a new colony starts in Auto.
- Where (Settings, Advanced, New colonies) can be Systems you hold (default), those and then 6 light-years around a colony, or 15 light-years around the capital.
- A planet needs at least 10,000 a month and a 60-month payback, or any positive value at Rich.
- Most colonies caps the count at Colony limit and administrators (default), or 5, 10, 15 or 20.

## Money and pace

- Money for new colonies is Money ladder by default. Then it needs Idle money and pays from the [Expansion rung](money.md#the-money-ladder), and Auto asks first while that rung is Recommend only (default).
- With a fixed line (over 5,000,000, 25,000,000 or 50,000,000), Auto launches without asking, and an expedition can use 10% of what's over the surplus line.
- It sends one expedition at a time (two at Endgame), 120 days apart at Idle money and 60 at Rich, once the last colony has finished its fast buildout or is 120 days old.
- In story mode with managed colonies free, it waits half as long and can have one more expedition under way for every two free (two more at most). With two or more free, it doesn't wait for the last colony's fast buildout.
- The Autarch administrator's free seats count as administrators, so a new colony doesn't need to hire one.
