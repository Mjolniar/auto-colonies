# New colonies (Auto-expansion)

## Turning it on

- New colonies (Settings, Playstyle) can be Off (default), Ask first or Auto. It'll only go after fully surveyed, unclaimed planets.
- On Money ladder (the default for Money for new colonies), Auto asks first while the Expansion rung is Recommend only (default), but a fixed line launches without asking.
- The Expansion page shows the proposal, expeditions and the best 5 planets, and it has Found now, Never this planet and Clear buttons.

## Where it looks

- Where (Settings, Advanced, New colonies) can be Systems you hold (default), those first and then 6 light-years around a colony, or 15 light-years around the capital.
- It skips Remnant nexus systems, systems another faction claims or holds, unexplored ruins, mission sites, the RAT Frontiers settlement, planets already targeted and your never list.
- Planets are ranked by their value a month at size 5, times location (same system x1.3, nearby x1.0, farther x0.7), minus 2% for each light-year from the capital.
- A planet needs at least 10,000 a month and a 60-month payback, but at Rich any positive value will do.

## Money and pace

- On Money ladder (the default), it needs Idle money and pays from the [Expansion rung](money.md#the-money-ladder).
- With a fixed line (over 5,000,000, 25,000,000 or 50,000,000), an expedition can use 10% of whatever's over the surplus line.
- It sends one expedition at a time (two at Endgame), 120 days apart at Idle money and 60 at Rich. The last colony has to finish its fast buildout or be 120 days old first.
- It won't start one during a siege, before a colony crisis or with a Nexerelin invasion coming.
- Most colonies caps the count at Colony limit and administrators (default), or 5, 10, 15 or 20.
- An Autopilot crew costs 85,000 of founding goods at base price, plus 40,000 and 2,000 a light-year, and takes 15 days plus travel.

## The new colony

- A new colony starts in Auto, with a goal that fits its plan (default) or one you pick. If it puts you past the colony limit, it hires an administrator at launch.
