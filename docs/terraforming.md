# Terraforming

- This needs Terraforming Made Easy (TME).

## Special worlds

- A colony plan can aim for a TME special world (Forge, Fortress, Paradise, Hive, Death or Waste) if the colony can get there. The steps follow TME's requirements, and the Unification Center goes last.
- Once a colony's paid for terraforming toward one world, it'll only switch to a plan that undoes it if that plan's worth at least twice as much.
- Auto colonies run TME projects one or two steps ahead, using up to 40% of the monthly budget. A 360-day structure only takes the construction slot when no better build's waiting.

## Surplus terraforming

- Surplus terraforming (Settings, Advanced, Surplus and money ladder) can be Max output (recommended), Pays back in 10 years (the Thrifty preset) or Off.
- It starts at the Surplus money tier and up, with the Economy rung On ([Money](money.md#economy)). A sequence that pays back within 24 months can use the monthly budget too.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

- Its projects are valued by the conditions they leave behind, and the colony panel lists the best four with Start buttons.
- Stations are covered in [Supported mods](supported-mods.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled).
