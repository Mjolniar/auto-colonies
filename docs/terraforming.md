# Terraforming

- Requires: Terraforming Made Easy (TME).

## Special worlds

- Plans: A colony plan can aim for a TME special world (Forge, Fortress, Paradise, Hive, Death or Waste) if the colony can reach it. Steps follow TME's requirements. The Unification Center goes last.
- Switching: After paid terraforming toward one world, a colony switches to a plan that undoes it only if that plan is worth at least twice as much.
- Projects: Auto colonies run TME projects, one or two steps ahead, up to 40% of the monthly budget. A 360-day structure takes the construction slot only when no better build waits.

## Surplus terraforming

- Setting (Settings, Advanced, Surplus and money ladder): Max output (recommended), Pays back in 10 years (Thrifty preset), or Off.
- Start: From the money tier Surplus up, with the Economy rung On ([Money](money.md#economy)). A sequence paying back within 24 months can use the monthly budget.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

- Projects: Valued by the conditions they leave. The colony panel lists the best four with Start buttons.
- Stations: [Supported mods](supported-mods.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled).
