# New colonies (Auto-expansion)

## Quick version

- **New colonies** row: **Off** (default), **Ask first** or **Auto**.
- Founds colonies on fully surveyed, unclaimed planets.
- A new colony past the colony limit needs an administrator.

## Turning it on

Settings, Playstyle, **New colonies**. With **Money for new colonies** on Money ladder (default), Auto asks first while the Expansion rung is Recommend only (default), and does nothing while it is Off. With a fixed line, Auto launches without asking.

Expansion page (Empire panel): the proposal, expeditions and the best 5 planets, with **Found now** (asks first), **Never this planet** and **Clear** (empties the never list).

## Where it looks

- **Where** (Settings, Advanced, New colonies): systems you hold (default); those, then within 6 light-years of a colony; or within 15 light-years of the capital.
- Skipped: Remnant nexus systems, systems another faction claims or holds, unexplored ruins, mission sites, the RAT Frontiers settlement, planets already targeted, and your never list.

## How it picks

Ranked by value a month at size 5, times location: same system as a colony x1.3, nearby x1.0, farther x0.7, less 2% per light-year from the capital. Minimum: 10,000 a month and a 60-month payback. At Rich, any positive value.

## Money and pace

- **Money for new colonies:** **Money ladder** (default) needs Idle money and pays from the [Expansion rung](money.md#the-money-ladder). A fixed line (over 5,000,000, 25,000,000 or 50,000,000) lets an expedition use 10% of the credits over the surplus line once those reach the line.
- One expedition at a time (two at Endgame), 120 days apart at Idle money, 60 at Rich. The last colony it founded must have finished its fast buildout or be 120 days old.
- No expedition starts during a siege, just before a colony crisis, or with a Nexerelin invasion coming.
- **Most colonies:** Colony limit and administrators (default), or 5, 10, 15 or 20.
- **Nexerelin colony fleet** (when Nexerelin has spare fleet request points): Nexerelin's price, points and time.
- **Autopilot crew:** 85,000 of founding goods at base price, plus 40,000 and 2,000 a light-year. 15 days plus travel.
- Both add the convenience fee at launch. **Method** can be set to Autopilot crew only.

## Administrators

Past the colony limit, an administrator is hired at launch (cheapest on offer, or a recruiter at 250,000 plus the hiring bonus). One is also needed if governing the colony yourself would take another colony under the stability floor. Nexerelin fleet colonies count toward the limit, and Nexerelin's free administrator is not kept. **Administrator past the limit** turns the hire off.

## The new colony

A crew uses the game's founding steps (Spaceport queued, 10-20% of the crew stays). It starts on Auto. **Goal of a new colony**: Fits its plan (default) or a goal you pick. **Fast buildout** (default On) keeps credits above the expansion line.

## When it fails

- Failed crew: price refunded, fee kept, planet skipped 180 days.
- Nexerelin refunds a cancelled expedition by stage. A lost fleet refunds nothing. Its system is skipped and the next 180 days use crews.
