# Terraforming

This needs Terraforming Made Easy (TME).

## Special worlds

- A colony plan can aim for a TME special world (Forge, Fortress, Paradise, Hive, Death or Waste), following TME's steps.
- Auto colonies run TME projects one or two steps ahead with up to 40% of the monthly budget.

## Surplus terraforming

It can be Max output (recommended), Pays back in 10 years or Off, and it starts at the Surplus money tier ([Money](money.md#economy)).

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

Its projects are valued by the conditions they leave, and the colony panel lists the best four with Start buttons ([Supported mods](supported-mods.md)).
