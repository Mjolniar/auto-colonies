# Your capital

## Which colony

- Capital: Picked by production gathering point, Storage contents, then size and accessibility. Change it on a colony panel or in Capital (Settings, Items and people). None turns it off.
- Capital does (Settings, Advanced, Capital and storage): Deliveries and commerce (default), Deliveries only, Off.

## Deliveries

- Custom production: Arrives at the capital, including [Shipyard](money.md#the-money-ladder) orders.
- Storage tabs: Weekly, ships, weapons, wings, blueprints, specs, supplies, fuel, crew and marines go there for a shipment plus 1,000 a ship. A refit or sell hub ([Items](items-and-cores.md#storage-logistics)) takes priority.

## Trade buildings

- Start: Net income over Trade hub starts at (100,000 a month; 50,000, 250,000, Right away), with no fast buildout running.
- Order: Spaceport, Megaport, Commerce, Trade Outpost, Bazaar, Requisitions Centre, Union HQ, Salvage Yards, Underworld, Waystation.
- Costly buildings: Need the Surplus tier, the [Economy rung](money.md#economy) On, and upkeep within 5% of colony net income.
- Station: Battlestation from size 5, Star Fortress from 7.
- Make room: From Idle money with slots full, the weakest ordinary industry is replaced when the swap pays, once per 90 days at most.
