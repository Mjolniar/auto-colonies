# Supported mods

## Quick version

- None of these are required. Support only applies to Auto colonies ([Modes](how-it-builds.md#modes)), and Diagnostics shows their status.
- If something breaks, let me know on [Issues](https://github.com/Mjolniar/auto-colonies/issues).

## Mods

- [Terraforming Made Easy](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29441.0): See [Terraforming](terraforming.md) and [Stable locations](how-it-builds.md#stable-locations).
- [Industrial Evolution](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=18011.0): Ambassadors, edicts (not harsh ones), VPC chips, the mining and refining specialty and Courier Ports are handled.
- [Nexerelin](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9175.0): Threat, sieges, the police crisis, the Derelict Empire start and colony expeditions are covered ([War and security](war-and-security.md), [Money](money.md#autonomy-nexerelin), [New colonies](expansion.md)).
- [Ashes of the Domain, VoK, ToT](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26307.0) and [Building Menu Overhaul](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=31308.0): Buildings are scored by effect, and research works as in [Research](research.md). Grand Wonders except the Daedalus Array, Star Citadels and custom production are left to you.
- [Emergent Threats (IX Revival)](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=27284.0): The Antimatter Stabilizer and the Panopticon are used.
- [Random Assortment of Things](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=26260.0): A Frontiers settlement is started at a 20,000 a month site, and the Expedition Hub goes where its fleets bring back the most.
- [Station Augments](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=30583.0): Output augments are picked while calm and combat ones under high threat.
- [Interstellar Imperium](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=8007.0) and [Knights of Ludd](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29124.0): Stations count like vanilla.
- [Grand.Colonies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=20986.0): Without it, plans have 12 buildings at most.
- [Substance.Abuse](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24378.0): The Brewery needs a recipe in Storage.
- [Volkov Industrial Conglomerate](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=19603.0): Viral contamination treatment comes first.
- [Roider Union](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9547.0): Dives and Union HQ are valued by deposits.
- [Planetary Shield: Access Control](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14990.0): It's valued as ground defense plus lower Pather interest.
- [Unknown Skies](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29876.0), [What We Left Behind](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=25205.0) and [Astral Ascension](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=28273.0): Mining conditions and water worlds count.
- [Niko's Scavengable Industries](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36195.0): Learned blueprints are built when they pay.
- [Junk Yard Dogs](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22826.0) and [Carter's Freetraders](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=24433.0): Forges and the Biolab are built normally.
- [Vulpoids](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29929.0): Organ Farms and Biotech are upgraded when they pay.
- [Yunru Industries, Slightly Better Tech-Mining, Yunru's Worlds](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=21968.0): The Bazaar only goes at your [capital](how-it-builds.md#your-capital).
- [United Aurora Federation](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=12709.0): The Dimensional Nanoforge, Servosync Pump, Modular Purifier and Garrison Transmitter are placed by effect.
- [Auto Outfitting](https://github.com/Mjolniar/auto-outfitting): Big weapon orders go through AotD production orders.
- [Kadur Remnant](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35145.0): Kadur Majority Faith and Virus Bombed count in build values.
- [JaydeePiracy](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36201.0): The Planetkiller Launch Site and item are left to you.
- [Corrupt.Officials](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35125.0): Extra income above the cutoff counts at the share the colony keeps.
- [Tahlan Shipworks](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14935.0) and [Gladiator Society](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=33586.0): No colony is founded on Kassadari Claim planets or The Gulf.
- [Scy Nation](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=29535.0), [CJY's Toy Box](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35093.0) and [AI-Retrofits](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=22261.0): Some of their buildings are left to you.
- [Blue](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=14041.0) and [Oculian Armada](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=9035.0): Lanestate Battleyards and the Orbital Matrix count as ship producers.
- [Cloning](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35130.0) and [Coalesced Little Mods](https://github.com/andrzejsokolowski/starsector-coalesced-little-mods): Their cloning buildings and the Megafactory are built when they're worth it.
- [Reverse Engineered Private Arsenal](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35627.0): Everything from it is left to you.
- [Toolbox Economy Curves](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36178.0): Plans use the curve you picked.
- [Sol Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=35983.0): Industries are priced for Sol's crisis.
- [Secrets of the Frontier](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=15820.0): Its habitat objective is left to you, and Warmind Refit is skipped while the Dustkeepers are hostile.

## Terraforming & Station Construction (Boggled)

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=17094.0)

- Terraforming projects are valued and started ([Terraforming](terraforming.md#terraforming--station-construction-boggled)).
- Mining and siphon stations are founded once researched, for TASC's price plus a fee. They're only recommended while New colonies is Auto and Expansion is Recommend only or Off.

## Omega Core Everywhere and Astral Ascension Renewed

- [Omega Core Everywhere](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36168.0) puts an Omega core from Storage where it does the most (no inspection points). With [Astral Ascension Renewed](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36167.0), one can govern a colony (10 inspection points).

## Rogue Planets

[Forum](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=36337.0)

- Every synergy pair counts in build values, except the Volatile Additive Reactor, where the second of Refining and Fuel Production is left to you.
- Fragile Biosphere worlds only get Farming as an industry, and Viable Slave Labor and Supercooled Cryo-Sinks colonies are held at 6 stability or more.
- Forgotten Vault-World's Tech-Mining, the Trash King's seat, the Violet Dwarf system and the Afterlight are left to you.

## Any other mod

- Deposits and colony items are handled like vanilla, and buildings with no formula are measured on a test colony ([How it builds](how-it-builds.md#mod-buildings)).
