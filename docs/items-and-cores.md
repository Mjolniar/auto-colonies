# Items, AI cores and administrators

## Autopilot Storage

- Colony items, AI cores, research databanks, Antimatter Stabilizers, VPC chips and recipes go in Autopilot Storage, and each colony item goes to the Auto colony where its extra output adds the most.

## AI cores

These settings are under Items and people.

| AI cores | Inspection risk | Hegemony inspections |
|---|---|---|
| Off | kept | kept |
| Safe (recommended) | Never inspected | Advise |
| Small colonies only | Small colonies only | Advise |
| Defended | More where defended | Resist where defended |
| No limit | No limit | Advise |

- Alpha cores (Settings, Advanced, AI cores and items) can be Only where removable (recommended), Anywhere or Never.
- Omega cores are only stored with [Omega Core Everywhere or Astral Ascension Renewed](supported-mods.md#omega-core-everywhere-and-astral-ascension-renewed).

### Storage logistics

- It's off by default. Once you set a refit hub and a sell hub (Settings, Advanced, Capital and storage), refit gear, known blueprints and specs go to them every week for a fee plus 1,000 a ship.

## Administrators

- Administrators (Settings, Items and people) can be Off, Place only or Hire and place (recommended). An administrator gives its colony's industries +1 output and costs 2,500 a month (20,000 with a skill) plus hiring.
- Past the colony limit, it places or hires an administrator for each extra colony right away, even over budget, down to the reserve.
