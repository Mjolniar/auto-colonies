# Low profile

Low profile is an optional expansion style (Settings, Playstyle, Expansion style). Colony crises come slower, and you'll earn less money.

- Crisis progress counts 10x, and Patrol HQ, Military Base and High Command are worth 10x more.
- AI cores stay out of colonies above size 3, and nothing goes up that takes a size 4+ colony to 7 Pather interest.
- For Tri-Tachyon one competing good is free, and a building making 4+ fuel is priced for the Diktat's interest.
- Free ports stay off, the War level is capped at Defend, and Omega and IX cores aren't used.
- New colonies only go in systems you already hold.
- Each faction's rule ends once you beat its crisis or take its deal.
